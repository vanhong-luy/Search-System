# Boss: Queen Bee

## Overview

- A pre-Hardmode Jungle boss.
- A giagantic bee living in a hive in underground jungle.
- Defeating Queen Bee is optional but unlocks the Witch Doctor NPC.
- Inflicts the Poisoned debuff through contact and stinger attacks.
- Features three distinct attack patterns.

## Spawn Condition

- Break a Larva inside a Bee Hive in the Underground Jungle.
- Use an Abeemination while inside the Jungle.
- Leaving the Jungle causes Queen Bee to enrage.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

- Alternates between three attack patterns:
  - Charges the player three times.
  - Summons Bee minions.
  - Fires poisonous stingers while flying.
- Use platforms or double jumps to dodge charge attacks.
- During the Bee-summoning phase, use piercing weapons to clear Bees while damaging the Queen.
- Keep moving during the stinger attack to avoid Poisoned.
- Honey pools provide increased health regeneration.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Charge attacks become significantly faster.
- Fires more stingers.
- Defense increases as health decreases.
- Contact damage also inflicts Poisoned.
- Stinger-inflicted Poisoned lasts longer.
- Shield of Cthulhu can completely negate charge attacks with proper timing.

## Recommended Weapons

- Weapons with piercing or lingering attacks perform well.
- High burst damage is effective while Queen Bee summons Bees.

### Recommended Melee Weapons

- Blade of Grass and Light's Bane: Works pretty well.
- Volcano: Good choice, but harder to get.
- Lucy the Axe: Alternative for Volcano.
- Terragrim: Can perform excellently.
- Starfury: Alternative for Terragrim.
- Thorn Chakram, Trimarang, and Flamarang: Long ranged melee, good at killing the Queen Bee.
- Amazon and Code 1: Best yoyo choice available.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Demon Bow, Tendon Bow, and Molten Fury: Recommended, best with Jester's Arrows or Unholy Arrows.
- Blood Rain Bow: Effective choice, best pair with Hellfire Arrows or Unholy Arrows.
- Musket, Undertaker: decent choice for the Queen Bee.
- Boomstick: A very powerful choice.
- Minishark: bBetter in Classic than Expert, best pair with Shark Tooth Necklace.
- Pew-matic Horn: One of the strongest pre-hardmode guns.
- Star Cannon: Infinite piercing and high DPS, but its ammo is more expensive (using star as ammo).

- Silver or Tungsten Bullets for guns.

## Arena Setup

- Fight inside the Underground Jungle or another Jungle area.
- A Bee Hive already provides a suitable arena.
- Add multiple rows of wooden platforms.
- Honey pools provide valuable regeneration.
- Rope can help dodge charge attacks.
- Solid blocks may block stinger projectiles.
- Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers improve survivability.

## Strategies

### Charge Strategy

- Dodge upward using platforms, double jumps, or Shield of Cthulhu.
- Slow or lingering weapons can be fired into Queen Bee's charging path.

### Bee Control Strategy

- During Bee-summoning attacks, use piercing weapons.
- Aim near the Queen's tail to eliminate Bees immediately after they spawn.
- Large-swing swords quickly clean up escaped Bees.

### Honey Strategy

- Use honey pools to gain the Honey buff.
- The Honey buff stacks with other regeneration effects.
- Flipper Potion or Water Walking Potion makes moving through honey easier.

### Magic Mirror Strategy

- Set a Bed near the arena.
- Teleport away when low on health.
- Heal, then return using a Magic Mirror.
- Queen Bee does not despawn when the player teleports away.
- DO NOT going outside Jungle, otherwise she will enraged.
