# Boss: Brain of Cthulhu

## Overview

- A pre-Hardmode Crimson-exclusive boss.
- A counterpart to Eater of World.
- Consists of two phases.
- First phase requires defeating Creepers before the Brain becomes vulnerable.
- Second phase focuses on dodging frequent teleports and defeating the exposed Brain.

## Spawn Condition

- Break 3 Crimson Hearts in the Crimson.
- Use Bloody Spine while inside the Crimson.
- Leaving the Crimson during the fight will cause the boss to despawn.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

### Phase 1

- The Brain is invulnerable.
- Defeat all Creepers to begin the second phase.
- The Brain teleports around the arena while Creepers attempt to swarm the player.
- Piercing weapons are highly effective against the Creepers.
- Creepers may drop Crimtane Ore and Tissue Samples.

### Phase 2

- Begins after every Creeper is defeated.
- The Brain teleports rapidly and attempts to ram the player.
- High knockback weapons perform well.
- Focus on dodging teleports while dealing consistent damage.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Creepers become significantly more aggressive.
- Creepers can inflict numerous debuffs:
  - Confused
  - Poisoned
  - Bleeding
  - Darkness
  - Silenced
  - Broken Armor
  - Weak
  - Cursed
  - Slow
- During Phase 2, the Brain creates illusion clones.
- Clones become more visible as the Brain loses health.
- The Brain also becomes faster at lower health.
- Damage-over-time effects and summoned minions help identify the real Brain.

## Drops

- 40 - 90 Crimtane Ore
- Bone Rattle
- 5 - 15 Lesser Healing Potions
- Brain of Cthulhu Mask
- Brain of Cthulhu Trophy
- If the Dungeon is missing, drop Dungeon brick (500-600 blocks)

## Recommended Weapons

- Piercing weapons excel during Phase 1.
- Fast, high knockback weapons are excellent during Phase 2.

### Recommended Melee Weapons

- Blade of Grass: Excellent throughout the fight.
- Blood Butcherer: Decent early alternative.
- Starfury: Piercing stars quickly clear Creepers.
- Falcon Blade, Exotic Scimitar, and Katana: Strong choices for Phase 2.
- Volcano: High damage if Hellstone is available.
- Rotted Fork: Best spear before Hardmode.
- Flaming Mace and Meatball: Excellent against Creepers.
- Amazon and Code 1: Strong yoyos for Phase 2.
- Bee Keeper and Hive-Five: Extremely effective if Queen Bee has already been defeated.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Tendon Bow: Best early-game bow.
- Gold Bow and Platinum Bow: Good alternatives.
- Molten Fury: Strong upgrade after obtaining Tissue Samples.
- Blood Rain Bow: Excellent against Creepers.
- Jester's Arrows and Unholy Arrows: Great piercing ammunition.
- Frostburn Arrows: Good budget alternative.
- Molotov Cocktails: Excellent area damage.
- Grenades: Strong but require careful positioning.
- Throwing Knives and Shurikens: Good piercing consumables.
- Spiky Balls: Effective before the fight begins.
- Bone Javelins: Strong against Phase 2.
- Undertaker: Reliable firearm.
- Minishark: Excellent sustained damage.
- Boomstick: Powerful at close range.
- Sandgun with Crimsand Blocks: Extremely effective due to infinite piercing.

### Recommended Magic Weapons

- Crimson Rod: Excellent support weapon.
- Diamond Staff: One of the best choices.
- Amber, Ruby, and Emerald Staffs: Good alternatives.
- Thunder Zapper: High damage for Phase 2.
- Gray Zapinator: One of the strongest pre-Hardmode magic weapons.
- Vilethorn (Corruption worlds): Excellent against Creepers.

### Recommended Summoner Weapons

- Snapthorn: Best whip available.
- Vasculash: Alternative whip.
- Vampire Frog Staff: High damage summon.
- Flinx Staff: Reliable early summon.
- Foxparks: Strong during Phase 1.
- Finch Staff: Budget option.
- Imp Staff: Useful for applying On Fire! and identifying the real Brain.

## Arena Setup

- Fight inside the Crimson.
- Build several long platform layers.
- Prepare the arena before breaking the third Crimson Heart.
- Clear nearby enemies using Purification Powder, Peace Candles, or Calming Potions.
- Block nearby cave entrances to reduce enemy interference.
- If using Bloody Spine, a surface arena is acceptable, but avoid building too high or the boss will despawn.

## Strategies

### Platform Strategy

- Build multiple platform layers.
- Use piercing weapons to quickly eliminate Creepers.
- Keep moving to avoid being surrounded.

### Surface Strategy

- Summon with Bloody Spine.
- Build a wide platform arena inside the Crimson.
- Stay below the Crimson boundary to prevent despawning.

### High Knockback Strategy

- Use fast weapons with high knockback during Phase 2.
- Repeatedly push the Brain away as it teleports nearby.

### Shield of Cthulhu Strategy (Expert Mode)

- Dash whenever the Brain teleports.
- Proper timing allows the player to avoid most attacks while dealing additional damage.
