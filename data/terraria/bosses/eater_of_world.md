# Boss: Eater of Worlds

## Overview

- A pre-Hardmode Corruption-exclusive boss.
- A counterpart to Brain of Cthulhu
- A giant worm made of multiple segments.
- Piercing weapons are highly effective due to its long body.
- Defeating segments causes the worm to split into smaller worms, making the fight more chaotic.

## Spawn Condition

- Break 3 Shadow Orbs in the Corruption.
- Use Worm Food while inside the Corruption.
- Leaving the Corruption during the fight will cause the boss to despawn.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

- Burrows through blocks and repeatedly charges the player.
- Damaging middle segments splits the boss into smaller worms.
- Head deals the highest damage but has the lowest defense.
- Tail deals the least damage but has the highest defense.
- Managing worm segments reduces the number of smaller worms.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Gains the ability to shoot Vile Spit projectiles.
- Takes greatly reduced damage from explosives.
- Above ground, moves faster and fires Vile Spit more frequently.
- Mobility becomes much more important than in Classic Mode.

## Recommended Weapons

- Piercing weapons are strongly recommended.
- Multi-hit attacks deal damage to several segments at once.
- Explosives are effective only in Classic Mode.

### Recommended Melee Weapons

- Blade of Grass and Light's Bane: High damage broadswords.
- Exotic Scimitar, Katana, and Mandible Blade: Fast swords that pair well with Shark Tooth Necklace.
- Ball O' Hurt and Flaming Mace: Excellent for blocking Vile Spit while damaging multiple segments.
- Swordfish: Spear that pierces several segments.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Demon Bow: Best early-game bow.
- Gold Bow or Platinum Bow: Good alternatives.
- Jester's Arrows: Excellent piercing ammunition.
- Unholy Arrows: Strong damage with piercing.
- Bone Throwing Knives: High damage against multiple segments.
- Flare Gun: Applies On Fire! to multiple segments.
- Sandgun with Ebonsand Blocks (corrupted sand block): Extremely effective due to infinite piercing.
- Spiky Balls: Deal repeated damage as the boss moves through them.
- Molotov Cocktails: Strong area damage.
- Grenades: regular, Sticky Grenades, or Bouncy Grenades effective only in Classic Mode.

### Recommended Magic Weapons

- Vilethorn: One of the strongest options due to infinite piercing.
- Gem Staves: Diamond, Ruby, Amber, and Emerald Staffs are strong early-game magic weapons.
- Wand of Sparking and Wand of Frosting: Low mana cost and applies debuffs On Fire! and Frostburn respectively.
- Gray Zapinator: Excellent burst damage if obtained.
- Magic Dagger (Remix worlds): Strong piercing alternative.

### Recommended Summoner Weapons

- Vampire Frog Staff: Offer strongest summon available.
- Flinx Staff: Reliable and hits multiple segments.
- Combination of 1 Vampire Frog and 2 Flinx provides excellent DPS.
- Abigail's Flower: Good when the boss coils together.
- Foxparks: Flamethrower attack deal high damage and pierces multiple segments.
- Snapthorn: Strong whip option.
- Soulscourge: Acceptable alternative to Snapthorn.

## Recommended Armor

- Recommended Health:
  - Classic: At least 200 HP.
  - Expert/Master: 300+ HP.

- Platinum Armor: Best general armor.
- Gold or Tungsten Armor: Good alternatives.
- Pumpkin Armor: Lower defense but higher damage.
- Shadow Armor: Obtainable by farming boss segments.
- Ancient Shadow Armor: Farm from Eaters of Souls.
- Fossil Armor: Best ranged option. Recommended to go with Gi.
- Jungle Armor or Wizard Set: Best magic options.
- Flinx Fur Coat with defensive armor pieces: Best early summoner setup.
- Gladiator Armor: Excellent for melee tanking.

## Arena Setup

- Fight on the surface whenever possible.
- Build multiple long platforms for movement.
- Stay inside the Corruption biome or the boss will despawn.
- Do not build the arena too high or the Corruption background disappears.
- Avoid building inside valleys.
- Place walls to reduce enemy spawns in Expert Mode.
- Campfires and Heart Lanterns improve survivability.
- Place Eater of Souls Banner in the arena to reduce Eater of Souls damage.

## Strategies

### Platform Strategy

- Build several long platforms.
- Force the boss to travel in straight lines.
- Use piercing weapons to hit multiple segments.
- Easier to dodge attacks than fighting underground.

### Minecart Strategy

- Build a short track with bumpers at the end.
- Ride back and forth while attacking.
- Excellent for piercing weapons.
- Use Ironskin and Endurance Potions in higher difficulties.

### Tank Strategy

- Focus on defense and survivability.
- Grapple to the ground or wear Gladiator Armor to reduce knockback.
- Stand inside the boss while continuously attacking.
- Thorns Potion increases reflected damage.
- More effective in Classic and Expert than Master Mode.

### Slimy Saddle Strategy

- Use Slimy Saddle to repeatedly bounce on the boss.
- Extremely effective when trapped between the boss and a ceiling.
- Can also be used in a vertical arena while attacking downward.
