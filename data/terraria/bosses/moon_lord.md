# Boss: Moon Lord

## Overview

- Final boss of Terraria.
- Spawns 60 seconds after all four Celestial Pillars are destroyed.
- Considered the hardest boss in the game.
- The message "Impending doom approaches..." appear.
- Possesses extremely high health, devastating attacks, and multiple attack phases.
- Consists of three vulnerable eyes (two hands and forehead), followed by an exposed core.
- Defeating the Moon Lord marks the end of the game's progression.
- Spawn Condition
- Automatically spawns 60 seconds after the last Celestial Pillar is destroyed.
- Can also be summoned manually using the Celestial Sigil.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

- Begins with three vulnerable eyes:
  - Left Hand
  - Right Hand
  - Forehead
- Hand eyes alternate between:
  - Firing Phantasmal Spheres
  - Summoning Phantasmal Eyes
- Forehead eye periodically fires the Phantasmal Deathray.
- Each eye only takes damage while open.
- After an eye is destroyed:
  - It becomes a True Eye of Cthulhu.
  - The True Eye becomes invincible and continues attacking for the - remainder of the fight.
- Once all three eyes are defeated:
  - The Moon Lord's Core is exposed.
  - The Core becomes the only remaining weak point.
- Occasionally uses its tongue to inflict Moon Bite, preventing healing from Vampire Knives and Spectre Hood.
- Moon Leech Clots spawned by the tongue can heal any remaining eyes.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Higher health and damage.
- True Eyes become much more dangerous due to increased attack frequency.
- Phantasmal Deathray becomes extremely lethal.
- Unlimited flight or excellent mobility becomes nearly mandatory.
- Dodging becomes significantly more important than tanking.

## Recommended Weapons

### Recommended Melee Weapons

- Solar Eruption: Best melee weapon for the fight.
- Daybreak: Solar Eruption alternative.
- Terra Blade: Excellent projectile damage.
- Flying Dragon: Powerful if multiple eyes can be hit simultaneously.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Vortex Beater with Chlorophyte Bullets: Safest ranged option.
- Phantasm with Ichor Arrows: Highest DPS but requires better aim.
- Aerial Bane: Strong alternative against airborne targets.
- Betsy's Wrath: Excellent support weapon during the Core phase.

### Recommended Magic Weapons

- Nebula Blaze: Best overall magic weapon thanks to homing projectiles.
- Nebula Arcanum: Extremely powerful when projectile stacking before the fight.

### Recommended Summoning Weapons

- Stardust Dragon Staff: Best overall summon due to excellent tracking.
- Terraprisma: Higher DPS but requires staying closer.
- Stardust Cell Staff: Good alternative.
- Constellation: Best whip available.
- Frost Hydra or Ballista Staff: Useful extra sentry damage.

## Recommended Armor

- Tier 3 Tavernkeep armor.
- A hybrid set is recommended.

### For Melee:

- Beetle Armor (Scale Mail for DPS, Shell for survivability).
- Chlorophyte Mask, Valhalla Knight's Breastplate, and Valhalla Knight's Greaves. This combination boasts incredible base defense and health regeneration, even outshining full Beetle armor in survivability.

### For Ranged:

- Shroomite Armor.
- Red Riding Dress combination if using minions.
- Shroomite Mask (or Headgear), Valhalla Knight's Breastplate, and Forbidden Treads (or Spooky Leggings). This gives you the high damage of Shroomite with much-needed survivability.

### Magic:

- Spectre Armor with Spectre Mask.
- Spectre Hood, Valhalla Knight's Breastplate, and Valhalla Knight's Greaves, turn player into a tanky mage, increase the survivability during the fight.

### Summoner:

- Spooky Armor.
- Tiki Armor if additional whip range is desired.
- Spooky Helmet, Valhalla Knight's Breastplate, and Forbidden Treads. This provides great minion buffs while the breastplate's health regeneration keeps you alive.

## Arena

- Build a long Skybridge (700-1000 blocks recommended).
- Add Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Honey pools along the bridge.
- Asphalt Blocks greatly improve movement speed.
- Place the Nurse nearby for emergency healing if desired.
- Remove nearby Floating Islands that may obstruct movement.

## Strategies

- Always keep the Moon Lord on-screen to anticipate the Phantasmal Deathray.
- Destroy all three eyes before focusing on the Core.
- Save mobility cooldowns for dodging the Deathray.
- Maintain constant movement to avoid Phantasmal Spheres and Bolts.
- Kill Moon Leech Clots before they heal the Moon Lord.
- Focus on survival rather than maximizing DPS.

### Skybridge Strategy

- Build a long Asphalt or Platform bridge.
- Continuously run in one direction while attacking.
- Fly over the Moon Lord whenever he teleports ahead.
- Healing stations placed along the bridge greatly improve survivability.

### Infinite Flight Strategy

- Use the Witch's Broom, Cosmic Car Key, or Soaring Insignia with strong Wings.
- Circle above the Moon Lord while attacking.
- Makes dodging the Phantasmal Deathray much easier.

### Nurse Strategy

- Build a house for the Nurse near the arena.
- Heal whenever health becomes dangerously low.
- Particularly effective with Stardust Dragon Staff or other passive damage sources.
- Can trivialize the fight at the cost of gold.

### Welcome Bomb Strategy

- Stack projectiles before the Moon Lord spawns.
- Phantasm or Nebula Arcanum users can heavily damage the forehead eye immediately after spawning.
- Provides a significant advantage during the opening phase.

### True Eye Synchronization

- Lower all three eyes to very low health before destroying any of them.
- Destroy each eye within a short interval.
- Synchronizing the True Eyes causes their attacks to line up, making them easier to dodge.

### Facetank Strategy

- Uses extremely high defense, damage reduction, and regeneration.
- Recommended equipment includes:
  - Valhalla Knight Armor.
  - Worm Scarf.
  - Frozen Shield.
  - Master Ninja Gear.
  - Charm of Myths.
  - Shiny Stone.
- Combine with Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Honey, Bast Statue, and regeneration potions.
- Mostly used in Master Mode with Summoner builds.

### Avoiding Moon Bite

- Use a Recall Potion just before the Moon Lord's tongue reaches the player.
- The Moon Lord teleports, but the tongue does not.
- Allows continued use of Vampire Knives or Spectre Armor healing throughout the fight.
