# Boss: Plantera

## Overview

- A Hardmode boss.
- Can only be fought after defeating all three Mechanical Bosses.
- The message "The jungle grows restless..." will appear after defeated all three mechanical bosses as the of Plantera's Bulb starts spawning in the Jungle biome.
- Spawned by breaking a Plantera's Bulb in the Underground Jungle.
- Must be defeated inside the Underground Jungle or she will become enraged.
- Defeating Plantera unlocks the Jungle Temple, post-Plantera Dungeon enemies, and stronger Hardmode content.

## Spawn Conditions

- Defeat all three Mechanical Bosses.
- Break a Plantera's Bulb in the Underground Jungle.
- Must remain inside the Underground Jungle during the fight.
- Leaving the biome enrages Plantera.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

### Phase 1

- Slowly chases the player using vine hooks.
- Frequently fires Seeds toward the player.
- Periodically throws Thorn Balls that bounce and remain on the ground.
- Seeds cannot pass through blocks in Classic Mode.
- Constant movement around a large arena makes most attacks easy to dodge.

### Phase 2

- Begins at 50% health.
- Loses its petals and reveals a large maw.
- Movement speed and damage increase significantly.
- Summons Tentacles that surround and protect the boss.
- Releases Spores that travel through blocks but can be destroyed with a single hit.
- In Classic Mode, destroyed Tentacles do not respawn.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Seeds can pass through blocks.
- Tentacles continuously respawn after being destroyed.
- Hooks also generate additional Tentacles.
- Dodging hooks becomes much more important due to increased pressure.
- Expect much higher projectile density throughout the fight.

## Arena Setup

- Build the arena inside the Underground Jungle.
- Do not remove too many Jungle blocks or the biome may stop counting as Jungle.
- Create a large arena with several platform layers.
- Leave a deep pit beneath the arena so Thorn Balls fall away from the player.
- Remove background Jungle walls to reduce hook attachments.
- Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Honey improve survivability.

## Strategies

### Kiting Strategy

- Circle around Plantera during Phase 1.
- Keep moving to avoid Seeds and Thorn Balls.
- During Phase 2, lead Plantera through a long tunnel while attacking behind you.

### Teleporter Strategy

- Build a long hallway with Teleporters on both ends.
- Teleport across whenever Plantera approaches.
- Forces Plantera to repeatedly travel across the arena.

### Face Tank Strategy

- Use Fetid Baghnakhs or Drippler Crippler.
- Stack defense, regeneration, and damage reduction.
- Hook onto a wall to prevent knockback.
- Continuously attack until Plantera dies.

### Minecart Strategy

- Use natural or custom Minecart Tracks.
- Recommended to build a diamond shaped tracks around the arena.
- Maintain constant movement while attacking.
- Keep the entire track inside the Underground Jungle to avoid enraging Plantera.

### Sentry Strategy

- Fill the arena with Ballistas, Queen Spider Staff, or Explosive Traps.
- Kite Plantera through the sentries.
- Summoner builds can defeat Plantera quickly with minimal direct risk.
