# Boss: Empress of Light

## Overview

- A optional Hardmode post-Plantera boss found in the Surface Hallow.
- Summoned by killing a Prismatic Lacewing at night.
- One of the hardest bosses in Terraria due to her fast, scripted attack patterns.
- Has two phases in Classic Mode and enhanced attacks in Expert/Master Mode.
- Defeating her during the daytime rewards the Terraprisma.

## Spawn Conditions

- Defeat Plantera.
- Find a Prismatic Lacewing in the Surface Hallow between 7:30 PM and 12:00 AM.
- Kill the Lacewing to summon the Empress of Light.
- Fighting during the daytime is your funeral, causes all attacks to instantly kill the player, but guarantees the Terraprisma upon victory.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

### Phase 1

- Uses a fixed attack pattern throughout the fight.
- Fires Prismatic Bolts that home after a short delay.
- Performs fast horizontal dash attacks.
- Uses Sun Dance, creating rotating light beams.
- Casts Everlasting Rainbow, producing expanding and returning stars.
- Summons Ethereal Lances that attack based on the player's movement.

### Phase 2

- Begins below 50% health.
- Attacks occur more quickly.
- Ethereal Lance becomes a full-screen sword barrage.
- Prismatic Bolts gain new attack patterns.
- Continues cycling through her scripted attacks until defeated.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Uses her enraged attack patterns throughout the fight.
- Attack intervals become much shorter.
- Daytime still results in instant-kill attacks.
- Learning the attack sequence is far more important than high defense.

## Arena Setup

- Build a very wide arena in the Surface Hallow.
- Use 3-5 platform layers spaced 15-25 blocks apart.
- Place Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers.
- Teleporters are optional for emergency repositioning.
- Keep the arena as open as possible to avoid getting trapped.

## Strategies

### Learn the Attack Pattern

- Every attack follows a fixed sequence and predictable.
- Memorizing the order makes the fight significantly easier.
- Focus on movement rather than reacting randomly.

### Keep Moving

- Constant movement avoids most projectiles naturally.
- Sharp direction changes help shake off Prismatic Bolts.
- Never stay still during Ethereal Lance.

### Skybridge Strategy

- Build a long platform or asphalt bridge.
- Running in one direction simplifies many attack patterns.
- Gives more room to dodge Sun Dance and Everlasting Rainbow.

### Minecart Strategy

- Build a looping minecart track.
- High speed automatically avoids many attacks.
- Only Ethereal Lance requires active dodging.

### Daytime Fight Strategy

- Not recommended for beginner.
- Every hit is fatal regardless of defense.
- Prioritize damage and mobility over survivability.
- Learn the boss at night before attempting the Terraprisma challenge.
- Ditch all defensive item and reforge them to Menacing.
- Use Soaring Insignia (dops from her treasure bag on Expert Mode)
- Phase 1 (Normal): She will alternate between firing bright yellow prismatic bolts, dash attacks, and the Everlasting Rainbow. To dodge the bolts, weave in wide, sweeping arcs instead of making sudden movements. When she dashes, use your mount to drop straight down or fly high over her.
- Phase 2 (Enraged): At half health, she moves faster and duplicates her attacks. Focus on running perpendicular to her Ethereal Lances and never circle tightly around her, as the resulting rainbow rings require a large turning radius to avoid.
- The "Flares" Cheese: If you struggle to dodge, you can use the game's 1,000-projectile limit. By repeatedly firing flares against a wall using a flare gun and summoning contact-damage-only minions (like Deadly Spheres), you can hit the limit and prevent her from using projectile attacks altogether, leaving only easily-avoidable physical strikes.
