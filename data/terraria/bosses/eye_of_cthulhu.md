# Boss: Eye of Cthulhu

## Overview

- A pre-Hardmode boss
- First non-optional boss
- A one big floating eye

## Spawn Condition

- May spawn naturally with 1/3 chance of spawning. The message "You feel an evil presence watching you..." will pop up when he about to spawn.
- Can be summoned using summoning item, "Suspicious Looking eye".
- Both natural spawn and item can be done only when at night (begins at 7:30 in-game time).
- Naturally spawn only when the boss has ont been defeated in current world or no altars exists in the current world.
- At least a player in the world has at east 200 maximum health and more than 10 defenses.
- At least four town NPCs (including the Traveling Merchant and town pets) are present in the world. Skeleton Merchant does not count.
- Will not spawn if player stay far underground until player return to surface at night.
- Will not spawn if player quit the game after the message pop up.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

### First Phase

- Float above player
- Summon it minions, Servants of Cthulhu, which fly towards player and deal damage, just like Demon Eyes, but can fly through walls and ground.
- After 3-4 minions spawned, it will charge at player, attempting to ram player 3 times before hovering again. Prepare to dodge.
- The Eye has 12 defenses during this phase, which negates 6 damage from every attack.
- Its minions are weak, can be killed fo Hearts

### Second Phase

- For last half of the fight, the boss sheds its iris to reveal a fanged mouth.
- Faster and deal more damage when it hits player
- It no longer has any defenses
- No longer summons minions
- Instead, it charges 3 times, pause a few seconds, then charge again.

## Expert Mode and Master Mode Fight

### First Phase

- Same as Non-Expert Mode Fight, but with some improvement.
- The Eye Spawns more minions
- After 3 normal lunges, it will perform a brief series of extremely quick dashes at a steeper angle.
- Enter second phase at 65% health instead of 50% and spawn 4-5 more minions in the process.

### Seconond Phase

- Same as Non-Expert Mode Fight, but some improvement.
- As its health goes down, it will lunges at player more frequently, until its only action is to dart haphazardly back and forth, making it rather hard to hit.

## Recommended Weapons

- Long piercing ranged weapon are recommended.
- A fast and steady damage dealer will help, as they can avoid the boss' damage negation
- When overwhelm with minions, switch to fast swing weapons liek a sword or flail will help
- Shoot at the boss' pupil with pierce weapon, as minions spawn from its pupil, so the weapons can damage both minions and boss at the same time. Spear is recommended.
- If not fighting on platforms, the Eye can go underground momentarily after a charge. Weapons like Whips and spears can hit it through the ground.
- Alternatively, debuffs such as On Fire! from Flaming Arrows apply damage even when the boss is out of reach.
- Keep in mind that bosses have minimap icons to increase their visibility.
- Take advantage of its transition tosecond phase, as it can still be damage during the transition.

### Recommend Melee Weapons:

- Long-ranged projectile swords: Enchanted Sword, ice Blade, Starfury and Blade of Grass are perfect, as they can shoot projectile, keep player distance from the boss.
- Boomerangs: can be great use, but missing an attack can leave player open. Thorn Chakram or Trimarang would be preferred.
- Yoyos: good choice with fine control. Amazon is the strongest choice. Alternatively, Malaise or Artey can be crafted. Rally is readily available, but need some luck and it's weaker than others.
- Flail: Flaming Mace or Ball O' Hurt is recommended.
- Biome swords: Light's Bane or Blood Butcherer are riskier but powerful options. Light's Bane spawn additional slashes, and Blood Butcherer adding a Bleeding debuff.
- Spears: Storm Spear, The Rotted Fork are fit for the fight. Trident and Swordfish are acceptable but slightly weaker.

### Recommend Ranged Weapons:

- Guns: Minishark and Boomstick are very effective, but rather hard to get. The Musket or The Undertaker are easier to get at this stage of the game, serve as weaker alternative.
- Bows: Gold or Platinum Bow are the best, as it cheaper and easier to make. But if player have Demon Bow or Tendon Bow is a better choice. Blood Rain Bow is great, but harder to find than other bow.
- Ammo: Silver and Tungsten Bullets for guns. Frostburn Arrows deal more damage to the boss, but Jester's Arrows can pierce through minion and hit the boss in one shot. Flaming Arrows is a good fallback options, cheap and effective.
- Bone Javelins: Do heavy-hit damage and add a damage debuff that stacks. Bone Throwing Knives is weaker alternative.
- Snowball Cannon: another recommend fast firing weapons
- Snowball Launcher: a heavy-hit immobile turret shoot without consuming ammo. Need the boss the stay in front of it.
- Paintball: Fast fire rate with high critical chances, but rare, and you have to kill The Painter NPC a couple times.
- Grenade: Good for damaging the boss, but can also damage player.
- Molotov Cocktails: Avoids the danger of self-injury and work very well, especially in the second stage when Eye of Cthulhu will charge at you through the flaming puddles the Molotov Cocktails leave behind.
- Others: Spike Balls, provides passive damage when scattered around arena. Shuriken and throwing Knives are effective. Poisioned Knives are similar but better.

### Recommend Magic Weapons:

- Gem Staves: simple to craft and long ranged piercing. Recommended are Diamond Staff, Ruby Staff, and AmberStaff
- Thunder Zapper: great, but no pierce.
- Wand of Frosting: easy to get.
- Crimson Rod: good weapon, as its clouds can be set up to clear out servants. Vilethorn is a slight downgrade, but useful if no other options.

### Recommend Summoning Weapons:

- Flinx Staff: Best choice alternatively, Finch Staff and Abigail's Flower. Vampire Frog Staff is even stronger, but harder to get.
- Foxparks: Strong option for killing the boss' minions.
- Whips: Cobwhip, Leather Whip, Slime Whip, and Snapthorn are choices. Cobwhip weakest among others, but easy to get. Snapthorn is the strongest choice, but harder to get.

## Arena Setup

- Multiple row of wooden platforms with enough room to dodge, adjust distance between platforms for player's jump (6 tiles recommended or depending on accessories player have or strategy player will use)
- Torches platform, bright enough to see clearly in the night
- Placing ladders (with platform or ropes) to aid vertical movement
- A long, flat surface is recommended, allow player to dodge the Eye by running to the left or tight with Swiftness Potion or with Boots.

## Strategies

- After making the arena, 6 popular strategy can be apply on: Slimy Saddle Strategy, Magic Mirror Strategy, Railway Strategy, Pathway Strategy, Facetank Strategy, and Lightning Strategy

### Slimy Saddle Strategy

- By using Slimy Saddle and bounce on the Eye and attacking it at the same time with ranged weapon.
- If doing this strategy, player need some falldamage prevention item, such as Lucky Horseshoe, Grappling Hook, an Unbrella, Fledging Wings or Cloud in a Bottle.

### Magic Mirror Strategy

- Attack the boss normally
- On the second phase, if player spawn point nearby, run one way and attack.
- When the Eye is going to charge, use Magic Mirror, Ice Mirror, or Recall Potion to teleport back to spawn.
- Now run the other way and repeat the process.

### Railway Strategy

- Build at least 400 blocks long of railway.
- Each end hammered into a bumper.
- When the eye spawns, start moving whilst shooting at it.
- Harder to build, but requires little mobility and take minimal damage.
- Best for single player or all players in a multiple world are together in the same area
- Alternatively, build the railway into a rhombus with around 200 rails. Best for keep the player out of boss' range most the time, make the boss even harder to hit player. Best to use with fast-firing weapon like Snowball Cannon or Minishark.

### Pathway Strategy

- use hermes Boots, together with the arena with 2 long pathways of platforms about 4 blocks apart, and the length is around 200-500 block each.
- Run along the 2 platforms attacking the Eye.
- Switch platfrom to dodge the boss attack and minions'.
- When reach the end of platform, jump down or go up onto another platform and switch direction.
- Recommended for Bows, Gem Staves, or Projectile melee weapons.

### Facetank Strategy

- A powerful, require minimal skill to pull off, but need a lot of preparation
- Need Platinum armor, an Ironskin Potion, a Bast Statue, food buff and full set of accesscories reforged/modified to Warding.
- After equip all necessary item, all boss' attack redues to 1 damage even in Master Mode, making you nearly impossible to die.
- Use any weapon of your choice.

### Lightning Strategy

- This strategy works only during a Storm.
- Fight the boss in an arena full of trees.
- When sparks apepar on top of a tree that signal lightning, try gmanipulate the boss position towards the spark and let the lightning handle.
- Works best when the boss spawing itss minions, as the Eye will be positioned above player around tree height.
- If successful, the lightning will take a chunk of boss health around 800-1200.
- Just becareful not to get hit by one.
