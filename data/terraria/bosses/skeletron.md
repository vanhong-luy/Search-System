# Boss: Skeletron

## Overview

- A pre-Hardmode boss guarding the Dungeon.
- Must be defeated to gain access to the Dungeon.
- Consists of a Skull and two Hands.
- Only the Skull needs to be destroyed to win.
- If not defeated before sunrise (4:30 AM), transforms into the Dungeon Guardian and becomes nearly impossible to kill.

## Spawn Condition

- Talk to the Old Man at the Dungeon entrance at night.
- Kill the Clothier at night while a Clothier Voodoo Doll is equipped.
- Must be defeated before sunrise.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

- Begins with two Hands protecting the Skull.
- Hands attack by swiping toward the player.
- After a short time, Skeletron performs a spinning attack.
- Destroying the Hands first makes the fight much easier.
- During the spinning attack:
  - Skeletron loses all defense.
  - Continuously chase the player.
  - Inflicts Bleeding on contact.
- Keep moving during the spin and attack aggressively while its defense is lowered.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Skull starts with 60 defense until one Hand is destroyed.
- Hands can inflict the Slow debuff.
- After one Hand dies:
  - Skull begins firing homing skull projectiles.
- After both Hands die:
  - Skull fires more skull projectiles.
- Spin attack becomes significantly faster.
- Prioritize destroying both Hands before focusing the Skull.
- Shield of Cthulhu and high mobility greatly improve survivability.

## Recommended Weapons

- Focus on weapons with good range or high sustained DPS.
- Piercing weapons can damage both Hands simultaneously.

### Recommended Melee Weapons

- Amazon and Hive-Five: Best pre-Hardmode yoyos.
- Volcano: Excellent damage and long reach.
- Starfury: Strong against the Hands.
- Phaseblade: Piercing projectile can hit multiple body parts.
- Blade of Grass: Safe melee option.
- Bee Keeper and Lucy the Axe: High damage, but require close combat.
- Thorn Chakram and Flamarang: Strong ranged melee choices.
- Ball O' Hurt and The Meatball: Best flails available.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Bee's Knees: One of the strongest choices.
- Pew-matic Horn: Powerful pre-Hardmode gun.
- Demon Bow and Tendon Bow: Best with Frostburn or Jester's Arrows.
- Molten Fury: Excellent with Hellfire Arrows.
- Blood Rain Bow: Situational but useful in certain arenas.
- Minishark, Musket, and Boomstick: Reliable gun options.
- Bone Javelins: High damage.
- Molotov Cocktails and Beenades: Strong consumable weapons.
- Star Cannon: Highest DPS, but expensive ammunition.

### Recommended Magic Weapons

- Demon Scythe: Best magic weapon available.
- Gray Zapinator: Excellent with Meteor Armor.
- Bee Gun: Strong homing weapon, especially with Hive Pack.
- Space Gun: Great with Meteor Armor.
- Thunder Zapper: Good alternative.
- Ruby Staff and Diamond Staff: Reliable early-game magic weapons.
- Crimson Rod: Free additional damage while using another weapon.
- Weather Pain: Excellent support weapon with homing projectiles.

### Recommended Summoner Weapons

- Vampire Frog Staff and Imp Staff: Strongest minions available.
- Hornet Staff: Good alternative, especially with Hive Pack.
- Snapthorn: Best whip available.
- Starcrash: Strong alternative whip.
- Houndius Shootius: Best sentry.
- Ballista Rod and Flameburst Rod: Good sentry alternatives.

## Arena Setup

- Build the arena above the Dungeon entrance.
- Avoid modifying the Dungeon floor itself.
- Create two or three long rows of wooden platforms.
- Leave plenty of vertical space for dodging.
- Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers improve survivability.
- Optional: Nurse and Dryad houses nearby.

## Strategies

### Hand Priority Strategy

- Destroy both Hands before attacking the Skull.
- In Expert Mode, the Skull has extremely high defense until one Hand is destroyed.
- Try to defeat both Hands around the same time to reduce skull projectile pressure.

### Spin Strategy

- When Skeletron begins spinning:
  - Keep moving continuously.
  - This is the best opportunity to deal damage because the Skull loses all defense.
- Avoid getting hit, as the attack deals heavy damage and inflicts Bleeding.

### Circle Strategy (Expert/Master)

- Build multiple long platform rows.
- Circle around Skeletron using:
  - Double jumps or Fledgling Wings.
  - Lightning/Spectre Boots.
  - Shield of Cthulhu.
- Bee's Knees, Bee Gun, and Space Gun work especially well during this strategy.

### Minecart Strategy

- Build a large diamond-shaped Minecart track.
- Continuously ride the Minecart while attacking from range.
- Greatly reduces the chance of getting hit.
- Molten Fury with Frostburn Arrows is highly recommended.
