# Boss: Queen Slime

## Overview

- A giant pink slime with a silver crown and a wing.
- A crystal can be seen inside her.
- An optional early Hardmode boss.
- Hardmode counterpart of King Slime.
- Can only be summoned in the Hallow.
- Features two phases:
  - Ground phase.
  - Flying phase.
- Summons multiple slime minions throughout the fight.

## Spawn Condition

- Use a Gelatin Crystal in the Hallow.
- Gelatin Crystals are found in the Underground Hallow near Crystal Shards.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

### Phase 1

- Jumps similarly to King Slime, but much faster.
- Regularly summons:
  - Crystal Slimes.
  - Heavenly Slimes.
  - Bouncy Slimes.
- Occasionally pauses before:
  - Performing a powerful stomp jump.
  - Shooting bouncing Regal Gel projectiles.
- Prioritize killing summoned slimes before they overwhelm the arena.

### Phase 2 (50% HP)

- Grows wings and permanently flies.
- Frequently fires Regal Gel in multiple directions.
- Repeatedly flies above the player before diving downward.
- Stay mobile and fight in open air to avoid projectiles.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Same pattern as Non-Expert Mode Fight.
- Minions attack faster and fire more projectiles.
- Constant movement and crowd-control weapons become much more important.

## Recommended Weapons

- Weapons with good crowd control perform best.
- Long-range weapons are recommended for Phase 2.

### Recommended Melee Weapons

- Shadowflame Knife: Excellent accuracy and bouncing projectiles.
- Bananarang: Very high DPS.
- Sergeant United Shield: Excellent crowd control during Phase 1.
- Amarok or Hel-Fire: Best yoyo choices.
- Ice Sickle: Safe ranged melee option.
- Dao of Pow, Chain Guillotines, or Drippler Crippler: High damage flails.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Dart Rifle or Dart Pistol with Crystal Darts: Excellent crowd control.
- Daedalus Stormbow: One of the strongest options with Holy or Ichor Arrows.
- Adamantite/Mythril Repeaters: Reliable alternatives.
- Shadowflame Bow or Marrow: Strong single-target bows.
- Onyx Blaster: Extremely high burst damage.
- Barrel Launcher: Excellent for clearing summoned slimes.

### Recommended Magic Weapons

- Meteor Staff: Extremely high DPS.
- Crystal Storm: Excellent sustained damage.
- Sky Fracture: Reliable all-purpose weapon.
- Spirit Flame: Very strong during Phase 2.
- Crystal Serpent: High single-target damage.
- Nimbus Rod: Free passive damage.
- Golden Shower: Defense reduction and piercing.

### Recommended Summoner Weapons

- Abigail's Flower + Firecracker: One of the strongest combinations.
- Sanguine Staff: Best summon for consistent aerial damage.
- Spider Staff: Strong during Phase 1.
- Cool Whip: Excellent during Phase 2.
- Firecracker: High burst damage.
- Queen Spider Staff: Effective against slime minions.

## Arena Setup

- Build a large platform arena or Skybridge.
- Multiple platform layers improve vertical movement.
- A platform-only arena allows bouncing projectiles to fall away.
- Thin lava layers can automatically kill summoned slimes.
- Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers greatly improve survivability.

## Strategies

### Crowd Control Strategy

- Always eliminate summoned slimes first.
- Weapons with piercing, explosions, or bouncing projectiles perform exceptionally well.

### Gravitation Strategy

- Gravitation Potion makes Phase 2 significantly easier.
- Constantly flip gravity while attacking from range.
- Shield of Cthulhu helps escape dangerous dive attacks.

### Skybridge Strategy

- Fight on a long Skybridge.
- Run back and forth while jumping over projectiles.
- Makes Queen Slime's movement much more predictable.

### Minecart Strategy

- A long Minecart track can easily outrun Queen Slime.
- Reduces pressure from summoned slimes throughout the fight.

### Ocean Strategy

- Use either Water Walking Boots or Water Walking Potions
- Fight over the Ocean using Water Walking.
- Most slime projectiles sink into the water.
- Keep moving to avoid Queen Slime teleporting onto you.

### Lava Strategy

- Place a thin lava layer beneath the arena.
- Lava automatically kills most summoned slimes.
- Use Lava Waders, Terraspark Boots, Water Walking Potion, or Obsidian Skin Potion to safely move through lava.
