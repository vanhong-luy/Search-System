# Boss: Duke Fishron

## Overview

- Optional Hardmode boss fought in the Ocean.
- Summoned by fishing with a Truffle Worm.
- Extremely mobile and one of the hardest bosses in Hardmode.
- Has two phases in Classic Mode and a third phase in Expert/Master Mode.
- Can be challenged early for powerful endgame-quality loot.

## Spawn Conditions

- Catch a Truffle Worm in the Underground Glowing Mushroom biome.
- Fish in the Ocean using the Truffle Worm as bait.
- Leaving the Ocean enrages Duke Fishron.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

### Phase 1

- Performs five fast charges.
- Alternates between Detonating Bubbles and Sharknado attacks.
- Bubbles explode after a short delay.
- Sharknados summon Sharkrons that attack the player.

### Phase 2

- Begins at 50% health.
- Charges become faster but only occur three times.
- Bubble attack forms a large circle around the player.
- Sharknados become much larger Cthulhunados.
- Defense decreases, allowing much higher damage.

## Expert Mode Fight

### Phase 3

- Begins below 15% health.
- Duke Fishron becomes nearly invisible except for glowing eyes.
- Teleports behind the player before every attack.
- Attack pattern follows one dash, then two dashes, then three dashes before repeating.
- Learning the teleport pattern is the key to surviving.

## Arena Setup

- Build the arena entirely over the Ocean.
- Use 3-5 rows of platforms about 50 blocks apart.
- Make the arena tall enough to fly over Cthulhunados.
- Place Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers.
- Asphalt on the bottom layer is optional for extra speed.

## Strategies

### Learn the Charge Pattern

- Duke Fishron always follows a predictable attack cycle.
- Dodge perpendicular to each charge instead of outrunning it.
- Save dashes for emergency repositioning.

### Manage Sharknados

- Lure Sharknados toward the arena edges.
- Avoid trapping yourself between multiple tornadoes.
- Inferno Potion can destroy incoming bubbles.

### Expert Phase 3

- Watch for the teleport before every dash.
- Memorize the 1 -> 2 -> 3 dash pattern.
- Prioritize survival over damage until an opening appears.

### Minecart Strategy

- Build a looping minecart track above the Ocean.
- Maintain speed while continuously attacking.
- Homing weapons work especially well.
- Looking up minecart rail build on the internet for this strategy is suggested.

### Teleporter Strategy

- Place teleporters on opposite sides of the arena.
- Teleport to avoid charges and reset positioning.
- Most effective during earlier phases.

### Chillet Strategy

- Equip Chillet mount grants player a dash that avoids damage.
- Player can dash throught Duke Fishron's charges perfectly.
- Chillet cannot fly, an tall arena with multiple layers of platforms is preferred.
- The Bundle of Balloons can give Chillet extreme mobility.
