# Boss: The Twins

## Overview

- A Hardmode boss.
- One of the three Mechanical Bosses.
- Consists of Retinazer (red iris) and Spazmatism (green iris).
- Both Eyes must be defeated to win.
- Defeating them drops Souls of Sight.
- Requires excellent mobility and sustained damage.

## Spawn Conditions

- Naturally has a chance to spawn at dusk after destroying a Demon/Crimson Altar.
- If spawned naturally, the message "This is going to be a terrible night..." will appear.
- Use a Mechanical Eye at night.
- Despawns at dawn if not defeated.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

### Phase 1

- Retinazer fires accurate lasers and occasionally charges.
- Spazmatism alternates between Cursed Flame projectiles and aggressive charge attacks.
- Cursed Flames inflict the Cursed Inferno debuff, preventing natural health regeneration.
- Focus on defeating Spazmatism first to make the fight easier.

### Phase 2

- Each Eye transforms independently after reaching 40% health.
- Retinazer stops charging and rapidly fires lasers that become faster as its health decreases.
- Spazmatism replaces its fireballs with a continuous stream of Cursed Flames, followed by extremely fast charge attacks.
- Avoid fighting both second forms simultaneously whenever possible.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Both Twins fire projectiles more frequently and recover faster between attacks.
- Retinazer's laser barrages become much harder to dodge.
- Spazmatism performs faster, more aggressive charge attacks.
- Mobility becomes even more important than defense.

## Drops

- Soul of Sight (25-40)
- Hallowed Bar (15-30)
- Greater Healing Potion (5-15)
- Twin Mask

- High single-target DPS weapons are preferred.
- Weapons with long range make the fight significantly easier.

## Arena Setup

- Build a long Sky Bridge or multi-layer platform arena.
- Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers improve survivability.
- Leave plenty of vertical space for flying.
- Asphalt Blocks greatly improve mobility if available.
- Minecart tracks are a viable alternative for ranged builds.

## Strategies

### Focus Spazmatism First

- Defeat Spazmatism before Retinazer whenever possible.
- This prevents fighting both second forms simultaneously.
- Retinazer's second form is much easier to manage alone.

### Retinazer Cheese Strategy

- Recommended for summoner.
- Defeat Spazmatism first.
- Once Retinazer enters Phase 2, hide inside a solid box of solid blocks.
- Attack through or over the walls with weapons like the Daedalus Stormbow, Meteor Staff, or summons.
- Retinazer's lasers cannot pass through blocks.

### Running Skybridge Strategy

- Run across a long Sky Bridge while continuously attacking.
- Use Wings to avoid Cursed Flames and laser attacks.
- Maintain distance to reduce incoming damage.

### Closed-ranged Unicorn Strategy

- Using Blessed Apple on skybridge and any kind of yoyo (Yoyo Bag is recommended) or long range weapon.
- Running with the Unicornand shoot yoyo behind.
- Player takes less damage, as the Unicorn is slightly faster.
- NOTE: The Twins may despawn if going too fast.

### Minecart Strategy

- Ride a long Minecart Track while attacking from range.
- Reverse direction when reaching the end of the track.
- Avoid moving too far away to prevent despawning.

### Gravitation Strategy

- Use Gravitation Potions to constantly change altitude.
- This makes it much easier to dodge Spazmatism's charges and Retinazer's laser volleys.
- Effective for all classes with long-range weapons.
- Alternatively, The Black Spot with medusa head also work. May result in taking more damage than intended.

### Sentry Farming Strategy

- Using Tier-2 equipment sold by Tavernkeep if available.
- The Twins much easier to defeat.
- Recommended to go with Squire Armor and using Ballista Cane.
- Using platform for your arena to maximize Ballistas fire opportunity.
- Heart Lanterns and Campfires are extra
- Any Wings and Speed Boots (Terraspark Boots recommended) with Cobalt Shield or upgrade.
- Summoner Enblem make the fight easier.

### Bounce Strategy

- If you are able to keep up with The Twins.
- Using Slimy Saddle pair with Meteor Staff to bouncing on them and rain down meteor on bosses.
- Not recommended, but fun to try.
