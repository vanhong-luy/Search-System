# Boss: Skeletron Prime

## Overview

- A Hardmode boss.
- One of the three Mechanical Bosses.
- Mechanical version of Skeletron.
- Consists of a head and four limbs.
- Defeating it drops Souls of Fright.
- The head must be destroyed to win.
- Limbs can block non-piercing attacks aimed at the head.

## Spawn Conditions

- Naturally has a chance to spawn at dusk after destroying a Demon/Crimson Altar.
- If spawned naturally, the message "The air is getting colder around you..." will appear.
- Use a Mechanical Skull at night.
- Despawns at dawn if not defeated.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

- Skeletron Prime consists of a head and four limbs:
  - Prime Saw (melee)
  - Prime Vice (melee)
  - Prime Laser (ranged)
  - Prime Cannon (ranged)
- The head alternates between hovering above the player and spinning toward them.
- During its spinning attack, the head gains increased attack and defense while all limbs become more aggressive.
- Prime Laser continuously fires accurate lasers and is usually the highest priority target.
- Prime Vice repeatedly lunges at the player and is the second most dangerous limb.
- Prime Saw performs slower melee lunges and is less threatening.
- Prime Cannon launches explosive bombs but is generally the least dangerous limb.
- Destroying the Laser and Vice first greatly reduces the fight's difficulty.
- If the boss survives until dawn, the head becomes enraged and is nearly impossible to defeat.

## Expert Mode Fight and Master Mode Fight

- The spinning head moves significantly faster.
- The farther away the player is, the faster the spinning attack becomes.
- Mobility becomes even more important than defense.

## Legendary Mode Fight

- Prime Cannon shoots will destroy tiles and potentially ruin an arena.
- To prevent it destroying tiles, use Dungeon Bricks. Placing storage item (Chests, or Dressers) on the arena's blocks or platforms will also prevent the arena from getting destroyed.

## Drops

- Soul of Fright (25-40)
- Hallowed Bar (15–30)
- Greater Healing Potion (5–15)
- Skeletron Prime Mask
- Skeletron Prime Trophy

- High single-target DPS weapons perform best.
- Piercing weapons can damage multiple limbs at once.
- Applying Ichor greatly reduces the boss's high defense.

## Arena Setup

- Build a wide arena with several platform layers.
- Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers improve survivability.
- Leave plenty of vertical space for flying.
- Avoid solid ceilings that limit movement.
- Frozen Slime Blocks can improve running speed without reducing mobility.
- Minecart tracks are also effective for maintaining distance.

## Strategies

### Destroy the Limbs First

- Prioritize Prime Laser, then Prime Vice.
- Prime Saw can be defeated afterward.
- Prime Cannon can usually be ignored until last.
- Once the dangerous limbs are gone, focus entirely on the head.

### Diagonal Movement Strategy

- Move diagonally whenever the head begins spinning.
- Diagonal movement makes it much easier to avoid the spinning attack.
- Stay within a reasonable distance to avoid triggering sudden rush attacks.

### Gelatinous Pillion Strategy

- Use the Gelatinous Pillion to bounce on the spinning head.
- The mount prevents contact damage while allowing continuous attacks.
- Particularly effective for melee and summoner builds.

### Summoner Focus Strategy

- Use summon targeting or whip tagging to focus one limb at a time.
- Destroy the Laser and Vice before attacking the head.
- Reapply whip tags regularly to maximize minion damage.
