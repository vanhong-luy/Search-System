# Boss: Lunatic Cultist

## Overview

- A post-Golem boss found at the Dungeon entrance.
- Summoned after defeating the four Cultists outside the Dungeon.
- Has relatively low health but uses fast, dangerous magical attacks.
- Creates clones during the figh:attacking the wrong one summons powerful additional enemies.
- Defeating the Lunatic Cultist begins the Celestial Events, leading directly to the Moon Lord.

## Spawn Condition

- Defeat Golem.
- Travel to the Dungeon entrance.
- Kill the four Cultists performing the ritual.
- The Lunatic Cultist will appear after a short summoning animation.
- Wait until he becomes vulnerable before attacking.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

- The Lunatic Cultist cycles through several magical attacks:
  - Rotating ice sphere that fires ice shards.
  - Three homing fireballs that explode on impact.
  - A lightning orb that periodically releases straight lightning bolts.
  - At lower health, he also summons Ancient Light projectiles that slowly chase the player.
- After several attacks:
  - The Cultist creates several identical clones.
  - Attacking the real Cultist interrupts the ritual.
  - Attacking a fake clone summons a Phantasm Dragon.
  - If a Phantasm Dragon is already alive, hitting another clone instead summons an Ancient Vision.
  - Fake Cultists glow more noticeably at night, making them easier to identify.
  - Continue damaging the real Cultist until defeated.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Increased health and damage.
- Below 50% HP, the Cultist begins summoning Ancient Dooms.
- Ancient Dooms fire dangerous cross-shaped purple projectiles.
- Clone mechanics remain the same, making accuracy even more important.
- Avoid weapons that may accidentally hit fake clones.

## Drops

Ancient Manipulator
Greater Healing Potion (5–15)
Lunatic Cultist Mask
Lunatic Cultist Trophy

## Arena Setup

- Reuse your Skeletron arena if available.
- Build 2-4 rows of platforms for easy vertical movement.
- Place Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers for survivability.
- Keep the arena open to avoid getting trapped by projectiles.
- Fighting at night makes the fake Cultists glow, making the real one easier to identify.

## Strategies

### Focus the Real Cultist

- Always identify and attack the real Cultist during clone rituals.
- Successfully interrupting the ritual prevents Phantasm Dragons and Ancient Visions from spawning.
- The clones has black squared eyes.
- Weapons with precise aiming or homing are especially effective.

### Staying Close

- Most of the Cultist's projectiles spread out over distance.
- Staying relatively close keeps attack patterns tighter and easier to weave through.
- Circle around him while attacking to avoid most projectiles.

### Avoid Area-of-Effect Weapons

- Large explosions or piercing attacks can accidentally hit fake clones.
- This immediately summons powerful additional enemies, making the fight much harder.
- Precision is more valuable than raw damage during clone phases.

### Prepare for the Celestial Events

- The fight itself is fairly short compared to previous bosses.
- Defeating the Lunatic Cultist immediately begins the Celestial Events, so ensure you have enough potions, ammunition, and durability before starting.
- Avoid leaving the area after the fight, as the next stage of progression begins immediately.
