# Boss: Wall of Flesh

## Overview

- A wall made out of flesh, sliding left to right, or vice versa.
- The final boss of Pre-Hardmode.
- Defeating it permanently activates Hardmode.
- Consists of two Eyes and a Mouth.
- Continuously moves toward the player throughout the fight.
- Must be fought in the Underworld.

## Spawn Condition

- Throw a Guide Voodoo Doll into lava while the Guide is alive.
- Alternatively, kill the Guide with lava in the Underworld.
- The Wall's direction depends on where it is summoned:
  - Left side of the world, it moves left to right.
  - Right side of the world, it moves right to left.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

- Constantly advances toward the player.
- Never allow the Wall to pass you.
- Starts with multiple Hungry attached to the Mouth.
- Defeat most Hungry first before focusing the Eyes.
- Prioritize the Eyes over the Mouth since they have much lower defense.
- Leeches spawn throughout the fight and can drop Hearts.
- The Wall moves faster as its HP decreases.
- Jump to dodge laser attacks.
- Heartreach Potion greatly improves healing from dropped Hearts.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Hungry respawn throughout the fight.
- Lasers fire much faster with fewer gaps.
- The Wall reaches very high movement speed near death.
- Piercing weapons become even more valuable.
- Lightning Boots and Shield of Cthulhu greatly improve survivability.

## Legendary Mode Fight

- Spawn a large amount of Imps, whose fireball can't be destroyed by attacking them.
- Hell Bridge made of Minecart Tracks is recommended, since they can't spawn on the bridge itself.
- Use Roller Skates and a Double Jump upgrade to prevent them from body blocking player, and make them spawn farther.

## Recommended Weapons

- Piercing weapons perform exceptionally well.
- Weapons capable of hitting multiple body parts deal the highest DPS.
- Focus on clearing Hungry while damaging the Eyes.

### Recommended Melee Weapons

- Night's Edge: Best melee weapon for the fight.
- Sunfury: Excellent piercing flail.
- Dark Lance: High DPS against individual segments.
- Volcano: Strong alternative.
- Starfury: Safe long-range melee option.
- Hive-Five: Best yoyo if using yoyos.
- Avoid most yoyos, as Hungry can block attacks.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Hellwing Bow: Best bow, especially with Wooden Arrows.
- Bee's Knees: Excellent alternative.
- Phoenix Blaster: Best gun available.
- Quad-Barrel Shotgun: Strong at close range.
- Beenades: Extremely effective, especially with Hive Pack.
- Star Cannon: Highest DPS but expensive to use.
- Grenades or Dynamite: Excellent for clearing Hungry.
- Meteor Shot: Best ammunition for Phoenix Blaster.

### Recommended Magic Weapons

- Demon Scythe: Best magic weapon.
- Water Bolt: Extremely strong with ceiling bounces.
- Aqua Scepter: High sustained DPS.
- Gray Zapinator: Excellent with Meteor Armor.
- Flamelash: Reliable ranged option.

### Recommended Summoner Weapons

- Vampire Frog Staff + Flinx Staff + Imp Staff: Best minion combination.
- Spinal Tap: Best whip.
- Starcrash: Excellent alternative.
- Retarget summons onto the Eyes after clearing Hungry.

## Arena Setup

- Build a Hellbridge around 1000 blocks or longer.
- Platforms or solid blocks both work.
- Solid blocks reduce laser attacks by blocking the lower Eye.
- Clear buildings, ash piles, and obstacles beforehand.
- Lava Waders, Terraspark Boots, or Water Walking Potions can replace a full bridge.
- Campfires and Heart Lanterns improve survivability.
- Drain lava pools from Lava Slimes if playing Expert Mode.

## Strategies

### Hungry Control Strategy

- Eliminate most Hungry early using piercing weapons.
- Hungry and Leeches frequently drop Hearts.
- Use Heartreach Potion to collect Hearts more easily.
- After Hungry are mostly gone, focus the Eyes.

### Eye Priority Strategy

- Attack the Eyes instead of the Mouth.
- Eyes have much lower defense.
- Weapons capable of hitting both Eyes simultaneously deal massive DPS.

### Mobility Strategy

- Keep moving forward at all times.
- Never let the Wall overtake you.
- Jump frequently to dodge lasers.
- Save movement abilities for the final phase when the Wall reaches maximum speed.

### Potion Strategy

- Water Walking Potion and Obsidian Skin Potion allow fighting without a Hellbridge.
- Featherfall Potion helps clear buildings and lava.
- Perform a practice run beforehand to remove obstacles.

### Boulder Strategy

- Place wired Boulders near the start of the Hellbridge.
- Trigger them as the fight begins for free opening damage.
- Can also use Dynamite traps for a stronger opening.
