# Boss: Deerclops

## Overview

- A pre-Hardmode crossover boss from _Don't Starve_.
- Optional boss that does not affect game progression.
- Drops several useful Don't Starve-themed items, including the Eye Bone pet.
- Can inflict Slow, Chilled, and Frozen-related effects.
- Has several long-range attacks and punishes players who stay too far away.

## Spawn Condition

- Use a Deer Thing in the Snow biome.
- Deer Thing is crafted using:
  - 3 Flinx Fur.
  - 5 Demonite Ore or Crimtane Ore.
  - 1 Lens.
- Can also naturally spawn at exactly midnight during a Blizzard if at least one player has:
  - 200 HP, or
  - 9 Defense.
- After spawning, Deerclops can be fought in any biome.
- Recommended to defeat the Eater of Worlds or Brain of Cthulhu before fighting Deerclops.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

- Uses four primary attacks:
  - Ice Spike waves.
  - Falling debris.
  - Roar that inflicts Slow.
  - Shadow Hands when players stay above it.
- Standing too far away (over 30 tiles):
  - Deerclops becomes invulnerable.
  - Teleports closer to the player.
- Ice Spikes freeze the player briefly.
- If blocked by terrain, Deerclops can phase through blocks to reach the player.
- Stay relatively close and keep moving to avoid its attacks.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Gains a fifth attack:
  - Periodically summons aggressive Shadow Hands.
- Shadow Hands:
  - Predict player movement.
  - Become more frequent as Deerclops loses health.
- Shield of Cthulhu and strong mobility become much more valuable.

## Recommended Weapons

- High sustained DPS works best.
- Deerclops has low defense, making most weapons effective.

### Recommended Melee Weapons

- Volcano: Best overall melee weapon.
- Bee Keeper and Blade of Grass: Strong alternatives.
- Terragrim: Excellent if obtained.
- Starfury: Strong ranged melee.
- Flamarang: Safe ranged melee option.
- Hive-Five: Best yoyo for this fight.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Molten Fury and Bee's Knees: Best bows.
- Demon Bow and Tendon Bow: Reliable alternatives.
- Blood Rain Bow: Strong with Shimmer Arrows.
- Musket, Undertaker, Boomstick, and Minishark: Good gun choices.
- Star Cannon: Extremely powerful but expensive to use.
- Silver or Tungsten Bullets are recommended.

### Recommended Magic Weapons

- Demon Scythe: Best magic weapon available.
- Gray Zapinator and Space Gun: Excellent with Meteor Armor.
- Crimson Rod: Cast once, then switch weapons.
- Thunder Zapper: High damage, high mana cost.
- Bee Gun: Great homing weapon.
- Vilethorn: Effective because of Deerclops' low defense.
- Ruby Staff, Amber Staff, and Diamond Staff: Good alternatives.

### Recommended Summoner Weapons

- Vampire Frog Staff and Flinx Staff: Strongest minion combination.
- Mushroom Staff: Good when using only one minion type.
- Imp Staff: Strong alternative.
- Snapthorn: Best whip.
- Starcrash: Better with high-damage minions.
- Soulscourge and Vasculash: Good whip alternatives.
- Ballista Rod and Explosive Trap Rod: Excellent sentries due to Deerclops' slow movement.

## Arena Setup

- Clear a large flat area.
- One or two rows of wooden platforms are sufficient.
- Avoid building too high, as Deerclops will phase through blocks.
- Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers improve survivability.

## Strategies

### Legs Strategy

- Stand underneath Deerclops' torso between its legs.
- Most Ice Spike attacks will miss.
- Only the third Ice Spike wave and Shadow Hands require dodging.
- Adding one or two platform rows makes movement easier.

### Short Skybridge Strategy

- Build a skybridge about 20-30 blocks above the ground.
- Deerclops follows underneath instead of using Ice Spikes.
- It mainly throws debris and summons Shadow Hands.
- Walk slowly ahead of Deerclops and reverse direction whenever it throws debris.

### Respawn Strategy

- Place a Bed near the arena.
- Deerclops does not immediately despawn after player death.
- Respawn, return to the fight, and slowly wear it down.
- Very effective with weaker equipment, though the fight will take much longer.
