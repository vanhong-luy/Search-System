# Boss: Golem

## Overview

- A Hardmode boss found at the end of the Lihzahrd Temple.
- Summoned using a Lihzahrd Power Cell at the Lihzahrd Altar.
- Has multiple attackable body parts across two phases.
- Possesses high health and defense, but relatively slow movement.
- Arena size inside the Temple greatly affects the fight's difficulty.
- Defeating Golem unlocks stronger post-Plantera progression.

## Spawn Conditions

- Defeat Plantera to gain access to the Lihzahrd Temple (plantera dropped Temple Key upon death).
- Use a Lihzahrd Power Cell at the Lihzahrd Altar.
- Can only be fought inside the Temple unless the altar is relocated.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

### Phase 1

- Golem walks and jumps around the arena.
- Both fists periodically extend to punch the player from long range.
- The head constantly shoots bouncing fireballs that inflict **On Fire!**
- At 50% head health, the head begins firing Eye Lasers that increase in frequency as its health decreases.
- Destroying the fists is optional but highly recommended to reduce pressure in later phases.
- Defeat the head to begin Phase 2.

### Phase 2

- The body becomes the main target.
- The detached head flies freely while continuing to fire Eye Lasers.
- The body repeatedly jumps toward the player.
- If the fists remain alive, they attack much faster than before.
- Laser frequency increases again as the body's health decreases.
- Defeat the body to win the fight.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Golem gains increased health and damage.
- Eye Lasers and fist attacks become much more punishing.
- Multiplayer significantly increases the danger due to much faster fist attacks.
- Prioritize mobility and avoid standing near walls where fists can trap you.

## Arena Setup

- Build a platform about 25-30 blocks above the ground to avoid most jumps.
- Leave enough vertical room to dodge lasers.
- If possible, remove unnecessary obstacles from the chamber.
- Place Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers.
- Lihzahrd and Flying Snake Banners help reduce enemy interference.
- Since the Temple cannot be reshaped before obtaining the Picksaw, work with the existing room layout.

## Strategies

### Destroy the Arms First

- Eliminate both fists before focusing entirely on the head.
- This greatly reduces the danger during Phase 2.
- Avoid standing close to walls where punches are harder to dodge.

### Focus the Head

- Prioritize damaging the head until it is destroyed.
- During the laser phase, maximize DPS to shorten the increasing laser barrage.
- Stay mobile to avoid bouncing fireballs and Eye Lasers.

### Platform Strategy

- Fight from an elevated platform.
- Golem cannot jump high enough to consistently reach the player.
- This makes dodging fireballs and jumps much easier.

### Teleporter Strategy

- Place two Teleporters at opposite ends of the arena.
- Teleport whenever Golem approaches.
- This greatly reduces contact damage and avoids fist attacks.

### Bunny Cannon Strategy

- Position yourself where Golem cannot easily reach.
- Fire explosive bunnies continuously.
- High burst damage can defeat Golem quickly with minimal risk.
