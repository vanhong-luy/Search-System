# Boss: The Destroyer

## Overview

- A Hardmode boss.
- A mechanical worm boss.
- One of the three Mechanical Bosses.
- The improved version of the Eater of Worlds.
- Defeating it drops Souls of Might.
- Highly vulnerable to piercing and area-of-effect weapons.
- Summons Probes throughout the fight that fire lasers and drop Hearts.

## Spawn Conditions

- Naturally has a chance to spawn at dusk after destroying a Demon/Crimson Altar.
- If spawn naturally, the message "You feel vibrations from deep below..." will appear.
- Use a Mechanical Worm at night.
- Despawns at dawn if not defeated.

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

- Burrows through blocks and attacks from below.
- The head deals massive contact damage.
- Body segments fire Death Lasers.
- Releases Probes as it takes damage.
- Probes attack with lasers but frequently drop Hearts when defeated.
- Stay above the Destroyer whenever possible to avoid being trapped beneath its body.
- Use piercing weapons to damage multiple segments at once.

## Expert Mode Fight

- Head contact damage becomes extremely dangerous.
- Laser pressure is much higher due to increased damage.
- Avoid direct contact with the head at all costs.

## Drops

- Soul of Might (25–40)
- Hallowed Bar (15–30)
- Greater Healing Potion (5–15)
- Destroyer Mask
- Destroyer Trophy

## Recommended Weapons

- Piercing and area-of-effect weapons are highly recommended.
- Balance boss damage with Probe control to avoid being overwhelmed.

### Recommended Melee Weapons

- Flint: Extremely powerful against multiple segments.
- Drippler Crippler and Dao of Pow: Excellent multi-hit damage.
- Fetid Baghnakhs: Extremely high DPS at close range.
- Shadowflame Knife: Safe ranged melee option with bouncing projectiles.
- Amarok, Hel-Fire, Chik, Yelets, and Code 2: Excellent yoyo choices.
- Flying Knife: Safe infinite piercing option.
- Mushroom Spear, Ghastly Glaive, and Obsidian Swordfish: Strong spear alternatives.
- Bladetongue: Strong close-range option.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Daedalus Stormbow + Unholy Arrows: One of the strongest choices.

- Phantom Phoenix: Extremely effective piercing attack.
- Onyx Blaster: Best gun choice.

- Barrel Launcher: Excellent crowd control.
- Star Cannon or Super Star Shooter: Extremely high DPS.

### Recommended Magic Weapons

- Crystal Vile Shard: One of the strongest weapons for this fight.

- Life Drain: Great against both segments and Probes.
- Meteor Staff: Massive piercing damage.
- Golden Shower: Good piercing support.
- Shadowflame Hex Doll: Excellent crowd control.

## Arena Setup

- Build a wide arena with several platform layers.
- Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Bast Statues, and Sunflowers improve survivability.
- Leave enough vertical space to dodge the Destroyer.
- Avoid getting trapped underneath its body.
- A sky box around 150 blocks high can block lasers, leaving only Probes to deal with.
- Running arenas also work well for exposing long sections of the Destroyer's body.

## Strategies

### Piercing Strategy

- Use piercing or AoE weapons to hit many body segments simultaneously.
- Focus on the Destroyer's body while eliminating nearby Probes.
- Do not ignore Probes, as they can quickly overwhelm the player.

### Vertical Movement Strategy

- Stay above the Destroyer whenever possible.
- When it rises toward you, fly upward, then descend while it turns around.
- This creates opportunities to collect Hearts dropped by Probes safely.

### Safe Box Strategy

- Build a small enclosed box with solid blocks.
- Lasers are blocked by the walls.
- Use weapons like Nimbus Rod, Clinger Staff, Meteor Staff, or Crystal Vile Shard to deal damage through or around the box.
- Only leave the box when necessary to collect Hearts or reposition.

### Tank Box Strategy

- Build a compact arena with Honey, Heart Lanterns, Campfires, and Heart Statues.
- Stack regeneration accessories and potions.
- High defense builds can out-heal much of the incoming damage while continuously attacking.

### Running Strategy

- Build a long Asphalt runway.
- Use Lightning Boots or better to outrun the Destroyer.
- Excellent for bows, Star Cannon, and other long-range piercing weapons.
