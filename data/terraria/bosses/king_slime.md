# Boss: King Slime

## Overview

- A pre-Hardmode boss
- A massive blue slime wearing a crown
- A ninja can be seen inside King Slime
- Low strats and predictable attacks
- Easiest boss
- Optional boss, but highly recommend, especially in early game

## Spawn Condition

- Rarely spawn on its own during daytime, and only on grass, replace a normal slime spawning
- Every 150 slimes defeated on Slime Rain event (75, if King slime was already slain in the world at least once)
- Use summon item, Slime Crown

## Non-Expert Mode Fight

- Hop onto player and deal damage
- A predictable jump pattern: two normal, one small, and one high
- Could teleport to player after the high jump or player getting too far away
- Become smaller and faster as he getting hit
- Spawn Blue Slimes as he getting damaged

## Expert Mode Fight

- Not much change from Non-Expert mode besides its higher damage and health
- Spawn Spiked Slimes instead of regular Blue Slimes
- Spiked Slimes can throw volley of spikee at short distance
-

## Recommended Weapons

- Any weapons should work, except for weak, close-ranged melee weapons like Umbrella
- Preferably Piercing weapons, to hit his minions and himself
- Have a grappling hook would help player during the fight.

### Recommend Melee Weapons

- Thorn Chakram and Trimarang: good for keeping distance from the boss, may struggle with minions horde.
- Storm Spear: pierce through horde and damage the boss with its projectile.
- The Rotted Fork: Better than Storm Spear, but lacks projectile.
- Ball O' Hurt, Flaming Mace, and Chain Knife: Give the player plenty of range, with the first two being able to pierce through minions as well.
- Mandible Blade: Can be thrown rapidly from a safe distance.
- Enchanted Sword, Starfury and Blade of Grass: Shoot projectiles, help the player avoid King Slime's heavy contact damage, though they are difficult to obtain at the point where you would likely fight King Slime

### Recommend Ranged Weapons

- Gold Bow, Platinum Bow, Tendon Bow or Demon Bow: cheap and powerful option.
- Arrows: Frostburn or Jester's are preferred ammo types, due to the high single target damage of the former and the piercing effect of the latter.
- Blood Rain Bow and Snowball Cannon: The best choice for the boss, but hard to find.
- Molotov Cocktails: have a large area of effect and burn enemy (debuff "On Fire!").
- Frost Daggerfish: If player done some fishing, this is the choice.
- Bone Javelin and Bone Throwing Knives: very effective for boss and good for clearing minions.
- All guns are effective against the boss.
- Preferred gun's ammo: Musket Balls, as Silver Bullets, and Tungsten Bullets are too expensive for early game

### Recommend Magic Weapons

- Gem staves: cheap, common, and do well againts the boss.
- Wand of Frosting: Also does well for this fight.
- Thunder Zapper and Crimson Rod: best choice, but harder to find
- Vilethorn: similar to Thunder Zapper and Crimson Rod, but not as strong as those 2.

### Recommend Summoning Weapons

- Snapthorn: Best choice, but hard to find.
- Cowhip and Leather Whip: weaker alternative, but easier to obtain.
- Foxparks: rare, but works well, switching between the flamethrower attack for minions and the normal attack (combined with whips) when attacking the boss himself. Foxparks can only be summoned in one slot, so any additional slots must be filled with other minions.
- Flinx Staff and Vampire Frog Staff: best summoning staff for early game.
- Finch Staff and Abigail's Flower: weak choice, efficient if no other choice.

## Recommend Armor

- Not very important, but recommended
- Silver or Tungsten armor: For all class, cheap and easy to find, increase survivability in Expert or Master Mode.
- Platinum armor and Jungle Armor: A better choice
- Gold armor: not recommend, unless no other choice, as its counterpart, Platinum is a better choice.
- Cactus armor: cheap, easy to get, has thorn effect dealing damage to slimes run into player. not recommend on Expert or Master Mode.
- Best melee choice: Gladiator and Ancient Shadow armor.
- Best ranged choice: Fossil armor with a Gi.
- Best mages choice: Wizard set or Jungle armor.
- Best summoners choice: Flinx Fur Coat pair with any high-defense helmet and leggings, Jungle armor preferrably.

## Recommend Accessories

- Most accessories can help. At this point, player unlikely to have access to Tinker's Workshop, so combined accessories are not revelant here.
- Boots: Hermes Boots, Duneriders Boots, Flurry Boots, or Sailfish Boots, will supports player's horizontal mobility.
- Fledgling Wings: not required, but great if obtained.
- Any double jump accessories: Cloud in a Bottle, Blizzard in a Bottle, Sandstorm in a Bottle Tsunami in a Bottle and Fart in a Jar.
- Band of Regeneration: Boost health regeneration.
- Feral Claws: great for melee and summoners
- White String: Add range to yoyos, if player use it.
- Shackle: +1 defend. it is easy to find.

## Recommend Potions

- Ironskin Potions and Regeneration: Boost defend and regen rate, increase survivability.
- Swiftness Potions: Increase mobility.
- Ale or Sake: Best for melee users.
- Archery Potions: For ranged users, increase bow's effectiveness
- Mana Regeneration Potions and Magic Power Potions: vgery important for mages for quick mana regen and magic power.
- Summoning Potions: for summoners to get an extra minions.

## Arena

- King Slime lacks the ability to go through blocks, and he will drop off platforms when player does.
- Player can make exploit by making 1 or 2 long rows of platforms above the ground.

## Strategies

- 3 popular strategy to beat King Slime: Platform/Minecart Strategy, Lava Pit Strategy, and Ocean Strategy.

### Platform Strategy

- Make 1-2 row of platform, about 18-20 blocks form the ground, and 35-50 long. Walk back and forth along the platform, firing at King SlimeIf he or his slimes reach the platform, drop down temporarily and use hooks or double-jumps to return; this will cause King Slime and his minions to drop down as well, following you.

### Minecart Strategy

- Same as Platform, but instead of walking, you make a rail for minecart. It takes more work, but provide a great advantages, and you can even use it for future boss. If you can't make a very long track, put bumpers on the end. King Slime will have a hard time trying to teleport to the player because of the speed attained on a Minecart.

### Lava Strategy

- Lava can damage King Slime. Use a Lava Bucket to make a long strip of land with a small amount of lava (shallow enough so items don't burn) and then make some widely placed platforms. Uses grappling hook to move between platforms, just not to jump into lava, unless you have Obsidian Skin Potions. King Slime will teleports, and die from falling in the lava along with his minions. The player doesn't have to focus on fighting, because the lava will dish out major damage. The player can also skip the platforms and use a Obsidian Skin Potion, and just evade by running under King Slime, or use Rocket Boots to fly over.

### Ocean Strategy

- King Slime moves very slow in liquid. If player can move faster than him, player can land free hit on the boss. The best option for moving in the water is the Hardy Saddle, obtained by fishing, but other options include the Flipper, Flipper Potion, or the Trident.
