# Terraria Boss Progression

## Pre-Hardmode
- Bosses fought before defeating the Wall of Flesh
- Recommended order: King Slime -> Eye of Cthulhu -> Brain of Cthulhu or Eater of Worlds -> Queen Bee -> Skeletron -> Wall of Flesh
- Wall of Flesh must be fought in the Underworld and triggers Hardmode on defeat

## Post-Wall of Flesh, Pre-Mechanical Bosses
- Hardmode begins; new ore tiers (Cobalt/Palladium, Mythril/Orichalcum, Adamantite/Titanium) become minable
- The Hallow and a second Corruption/Crimson biome spread across the world
- Recommended prep: Hardmode ore armor/weapons before attempting the Mechanical Bosses

## Post-Mechanical Bosses (The Twins, The Destroyer, Skeletron Prime), Pre-Plantera
- Any one (or all three) Mechanical Bosses defeated
- Unlocks Hallowed/Chlorophyte-adjacent gear tiers and access to Plantera's Bulb in the Underground Jungle

## Post-Plantera, Pre-Golem
- Plantera defeated; Temple in the Underground Jungle unlocks
- Golem is the next intended boss, fought inside the Jungle Temple

## Post-Golem, Pre-Moon Lord
- Golem defeated; endgame prep phase
- Duke Fishron (fishable, not gated by Golem) and Empress of Light (Hallow, night/day) are commonly fought around here
- Lunar Events / Celestial Pillars must be triggered and cleared to summon Moon Lord

## Post-Moon Lord
- Endgame content; Moon Lord is the final intended boss
