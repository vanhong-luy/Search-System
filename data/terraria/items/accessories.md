## Cloud in a Bottle

- Pre-Hardmode
- Found in Gold Chests in the Underground and Cavern layers. Also dropped by Mimics (Pre-Hardmode enemy)
- Grant player an extra jump.

## Papyrus Scarab

- Hardmode, post-Plantera.
- Cannot be found anywhere.
- Craftable: Hercules Beetle and Necromantic Scroll.
- Crafting Station: Tinkerer's Workshop.
- Increases summon weapon damage by 15%, their knockback by 2, and the player's maximum number of minions by 1. Stack with Pygmy Necklace and Necromantic Scroll.
- Stacks with both of its ingredients.
- Cannot be used to craft anything.

## Necromantic Scroll

- hardmode, post-Plantera.
- Cannot be found anywhere.
- Dropped from Mouring Wood (Hardmode, post-Plantera miniboss spawn during Pumpkin Moon event).
- Increases the player's maximum number of minions by one and increases the damage of all summon weapons by 10%. Stack with Papyrus Scarab.
- Cannot be crafted.
- Used in Papyrus Scarab.

## Fishron Wings

- Hardmode.
- Dropped by Duke Fishron (one of six items always dropped, each with a 16.67% chance).
- Cannot be crafted.
- Allow flight for a total of 3 seconds, reaching a max height of 143 tiles, with a top horizontal speed of 41 mph.
- Grant increased movement speed while in liquids (water, honey), leaving a bubble trail while flying.
- Cannot be used to craft anything.

## Betsy's Wings

- Hardmode, post-Golem.
- Dropped by Betsy, the boss of the seventh and final wave of Tier 3 in the Old One's Army event (25% chance).
- Cannot be crafted.
- Allow flight for a total of 2.5 seconds, reaching a max height of 119 tiles.
- Allow horizontal hovering by holding Down + Jump; hovering without pressing a directional key uses flight time at half the normal rate, effectively doubling duration while hovering in place.
- Cannot be used to craft anything.

## Amphibian Boots

- Hardmode.
- Cannot be found anywhere.
- Craftable: Sailfish Boots and Frog Leg.
- Crafting Station: Tinkerer's Workshop.
- Increases max movement speed to 31 mph, allows auto-jumping, increases jump speed by 48%, and increases safe falling distance by 15 blocks.
- Cannot be used to craft anything.

## Celestial Shell

- Hardmode, post-Golem.
- Cannot be found anywhere.
- Craftable: Celestial Stone and Moon Shell.
- Crafting Station: Tinkerer's Workshop.
- Combines Celestial Stone's stat boosts (melee speed, damage, crit chance, regen, defense, mining speed, minion knockback) with Moon Shell's Werewolf (night) and Merfolk (in liquid) transformations.
- Does not stack with any of its component parts, but does stack with Moon Stone and Sun Stone.
- Cannot be used to craft anything.

## Recon Scope

- Hardmode, post-Golem.
- Cannot be found anywhere.
- Craftable: Sniper Scope and Putrid Scent.
- Crafting Station: Tinkerer's Workshop.
- Grants +10% ranged damage, +10% ranged critical strike chance, a "zoom" ability while holding a bullet/Candy Corn/Gel/Stake-firing gun, and reduces aggro.
- Does not inherit Putrid Scent's damage/crit bonus (it's granted independently by this item).
- Cannot be used to craft anything.

- Craftable: Rifle Scope and Destroyer Emblem.

- Grants +10% ranged damage, +10% ranged critical strike chance, and a "zoom" ability while holding a bullet-firing gun.

## Destroyer Emblem

- Hardmode, post-Golem.

## Mechanical Glove

- Hardmode, post-mechanical bosses.
- Cannot be found anywhere.
- Craftable: Power Glove and Avenger Emblem.
- Crafting Station: Tinkerer's Workshop.
- Grants autoswing for all melee weapons and whips, +12% melee damage, +12% melee speed, 2x melee knockback, and +10% melee weapon size.
- Used in Fire Gauntlet.

## Soaring Insignia

- Hardmode, post-Plantera, Expert Mode exclusive.

- Grants infinite flight time when paired with wings or Rocket Boots, increases acceleration by 75%, and increases jump speed by 1.8.
- Cannot be used to craft anything.
