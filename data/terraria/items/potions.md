## Recovery Potion

- A must-have Potion Type
- Can heal and give player's mana.

### Mushroom

- Not a potion, but a quick heal item.

- Serve as base ingredient for healing potion.

### Bottled Water

- Found naturally growing on grass.
- Craftable: Bottle.
- Crafting Station: Stand in Water or near sink or water fountain.

### Bottled Honey

- Dropped by the Queen Bee, found in Bee Hives in the Underground Jungle.

## Food

- Any Food or Fuit grant player either Well Fed, Plenty Satisfied or Exquisitely Stuffed.
- Well Fed: Minor improvements to all stats (+2 defense, +5% damage, +2% critical strike chance, +5% melee speed, +0.5 minion knockback, +20% movement speed, +5% mining speed)
- Plenty Satisfied: Medium improvements to all stats (+3 defense, +7.5% damage, +3% critical strike chance, +7.5% melee speed, +0.75 minion knockback, +30% movement speed, +10% mining speed)
- Exquisitely Stuffed: Major improvements to all stats (+4 defense, +10% damage, +4% critical strike chance, +10% melee speed, +1 minion knockback, +40% movement speed, +15% mining speed)

### Cooked Fish

- Cannot be found anywhere.
- Craftable: Trout, Atlantic Cod, Bass.
- Crafting Station: Cooking Pot or Cauldron.
- Grant Well Fed.

## Ale

- Buy from Tavernkeep.
- Craftable: Mug (2x Glass, using Furnace)
- Crafting Station: Keg.
- Grant Tipsy debuff for 2 minutes, which provides excellent melee boosts but lowers your defense: +10% Melee damage, +10% Melee attack speed, +2% Melee critical strike chance, -4 Defense, +5 Fishing Power.

## Sake

- Buy from Traveling Merchant.
- Cannot be crafted.
- Grant Tipsy debuff for 4 minutes, which provides excellent melee boosts but lowers your defense: +10% Melee damage, +10% Melee attack speed, +2% Melee critical strike chance, -4 Defense, +5 Fishing Power.
