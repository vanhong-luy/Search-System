## Wizard Set

- Not a single crafted armor but a mix-and-match headgear + robe combo. Any leg-slot item can be worn without losing the set bonus.

### Wizard Hat

- Pre-Hardmode.
- Helmet slot item.
- grants +4 defense and +5% magic damage.
- Cannot be crafted; always dropped by Tim (a rare Cavern layer enemy).
- Part of the Wizard set: worn with a Robe (Mystic or Gem) grants +10% magic critical strike chance.

### Magic Hat

- Pre-Hardmode.
- Helmet slot item.
- Grants +2 defense, +6% magic damage, and +6% magic critical strike chance.
- Cannot be crafted; purchased from the Traveling Merchant for 3 gold.
- Part of the Wizard set: worn with a Robe grants +60 maximum mana.

### Mystic Robe

- Pre-Hardmode.
- Shirt slot item.
- Grants 2 defense, +6% magic damage/critical strike chance, and 10% reduced mana usage.
- Cannot be crafted; purchased from the Traveling Merchant for 3 gold 50 silver.
- Part of the Wizard set: paired with Wizard Hat gives +10% magic critical strike chance; paired with Magic Hat gives +60 mana.

### Gem Robes

- Pre-Hardmode.
- Shirt slot items (one for each gem type: Amethyst, Topaz, Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby, Diamond, Amber)
- Each granting defense plus magic damage/critical strike bonuses that scale with gem quality (Diamond Robe is the strongest).
- Crafted from 1 Robe and gems at a Loom (Robe itself needs 20 Silk).
- Part of the Wizard set: paired with Wizard Hat gives +10% magic critical strike chance; paired with Magic Hat gives +60 mana.
- Crafting Station: Loom.

## Titanium Armor

- Hardmode.
- Consist of Titanium Mask/Helmet/Headgear (melee/ranged/magic), Titanium Breastplate, and Titanium Leggings. Alternate ore counterpart to Adamantite armor (worlds get one or the other).
- Full set (one headpiece) grant defense slightly higher than Adamantite.
- Set bonus grants the "Titanium Barrier" buff on hit, spawning up to 7 spinning shards that each deal 50 damage.
- Crafting a one-helmet set needs 59 Titanium Bars (236 Titanium Ore); all five pieces need 85 Titanium Bars (340 Titanium Ore).
- Crafting Station: Mythril Anvil or Orichalcum Anvil.

## Adamantite Armor

- Hardmode.
- Consist of Adamantite Mask/Helmet/Headgear (melee/ranged/magic), Adamantite Breastplate, and Adamantite Leggings. Alternate ore counterpart to Titanium armor.
- Full set grants slightly more defense than Titanium (50 total with matching pieces).
- Set bonus depends on headpiece (e.g., helmet gives +20% melee/movement speed, mask gives 25% chance to not consume ammo).
- Crafted from Adamantite Bars.
- Crafting Station: Mythril Anvil or Orichalcum Anvil.

## Orichalcum Armor

## Tavernkeep armor

All four Tavernkeep sets are purchased with Defender Medals (earned from the Old One's Army event), become available after defeating any mechanical boss (Tier 1). Their upgrades are purchased later for additional Defender Medals after Plantera is defeated (Tier 2). Tier 3 unlocked once Golem has been defeated.

### Squire Armor

- Hardmode, melee/summoner hybrid.
- Consist of the Squire's Great Helm, Squire's Plating, and Squire's Greaves.
- Full set grant +58 defense, +15% melee damage, +30% summon damage, +15%/+20% melee critical strike chance, +20% movement speed, +2 sentry slots, and increased health regeneration. Set bonus boosts Ballista sentry speed/penetration.
- Purchased from the Tavernkeep.

### Valhalla Knight (Squire Upgrade)

- Hardmode, post-Plantera.
- Direct upgrade of Squire armor with similar melee/summon/regeneration bonuses but higher stats (+68 defense, +10% melee damage, +60% summon damage, +20% melee critical strike chance, +3 sentry slots, and increased health regeneration by 2); the Breastplate has the highest summon damage boost of any armor piece in the game.
- Purchased from the Tavernkeep for additional Defender Medals.

### Apprentice Armor

- Hardmode, mage/summoner hybrid.
- Consist of the Apprentice's Hat, Apprentice's Robe, and Apprentice's Trousers.
- Full set grants magic/summon damage bonuses roughly comparable to Hallowed or Chlorophyte armor, plus increased range, velocity, and field of view for Flameburst sentries.
- Full set grants +32 defense +20% magic damage, +20% magic critical strike chance, +10% mana cost reduction, +30% summon damage, +2 sentry and slots and +20% movement speed.
- Purchased from the Tavernkeep.

### Dark Artist (Apprentice Upgrade)

- Hardmode, post-Plantera.
- Direct upgrade of Apprentice armor with stronger magic/summon bonuses and further-improved sentry effects.
- Full set grants +42 defense +25% magic damage, +25% magic critical strike chance, +15% mana cost reduction, +30% summon damage, +3 sentry and slots and +20% movement speed.
- Purchased from the Tavernkeep for additional Defender Medals.

### Huntress Armor

- Hardmode, ranged/summoner hybrid.
- Consist of the Huntress's Wig, Huntress's Jerkin, and Huntress's Pants.
- Full set grants ranged/summon damage bonuses similar to Titanium/Adamantite ranged armor, plus increased fire rate and Oiled-debuff chance for Explosive Trap sentries.
- Full set grants +36 defense +20% ranged damage, +10% ammo conservation, +30% summon damage, +2 sentry and slots and +20% movement speed.
- Purchased from the Tavernkeep.

### Red Riding (Huntress Upgrade)

- Hardmode, post-Plantera.
- Direct upgrade of Huntress armor with stronger ranged/summon bonuses, added ranged critical chance, and ammo conservation.
- Full set grants +48 defense +25% ranged damage, +20% ammo conservation, +60% summon damage, +3 sentry and slots and +20% movement speed.
- Purchased from the Tavernkeep for additional Defender Medals.

### Monk Armor

- Hardmode, melee/summoner hybrid.
- Consist of the Monk's Bushy Brow Bald Cap, Monk's Shirt, and Monk's Pants.
- Full set grants melee speed and summon bonuses (trades some defense/regeneration versus Squire armor for more melee speed); boosts Lightning Aura sentry attack rate and gives it a chance to crit.
- Full set grants +46 defense +20% melee damage, +20% melee speed, +2 sentry and slots and +20% movement speed.
- Purchased from the Tavernkeep.

### Shinobi Infiltrator (Monk Upgrade)

- Hardmode, post-Plantera.
- Direct upgrade of Monk armor with higher melee damage/speed and summon bonuses, and further-enhanced Lightning Aura sentries.
- Full set grants +52 defense +20% melee damage, +20% melee speed, +25% critical strike chance, +2 sentry and slots and +30% movement speed.
- Purchased from the Tavernkeep for additional Defender Medals.

## Shroomite Armor

- Hardmode, post-Plantera.
- Consist of the Shroomite Breastplate, Shroomite Leggings, and one of three headpieces (Headgear/Mask/Helmet, each specialized for a different ammo type).
- Full set (any headpiece) grants +51 defense, +13% ranged damage, +25% ranged critical strike chance, +20% ammo conservation and +12% movement speed. Standing still and not using any items causes the player to quickly enter stealth, which causes them to turn almost invisible and prevents Fighter AI and Flying AI enemies from firing projectiles at the player while they are not using an item. Stealth is engaged gradually over the course of 1.12 seconds, during which the player gains several stat boosts that linearly increase to the following values:
  +60% ranged damage
  +10% ranged critical strike chance
  -750 aggro
  +50% ranged knockback
  Stealth mode is deactivated upon riding a mount or taking damage. Stealth mode will also fade once the player starts moving
- Full set (with Headgear) grants x1.12 damage of bows, repeaters, arrows, and Stakes.
- Full set (with Mask) grants x1.12 damage of guns, bullets, and Candy Corn.
- Full set (with Helmet) grants x1.12 damage of specialist ranged weapons (non-consumable ranged weapons that are not bows or guns) and their ammunition.
- Crafting a full set with one headpiece needs 54 Shroomite Bars (810 Glowing Mushrooms and 270 Chlorophyte Ore, made at an Autohammer bought from the Truffle NPC).
- Crafting Station: Mythril Anvil or Orichalcum Anvil (Autohammer for bars).

## Spectre Armor

- Hardmode, post-Plantera.
- Consist of the Spectre Robe, Spectre Pants, and one of two headpieces (Spectre Mask for offense, Spectre Hood for healing).
- Full set with Mask +42 defense +25% magic damage, +17% magic critical strike chance, 60+ maximum mana, +13% mana cost reduction +8% movement speed and fires homing damage orbs on magic hits.
- Full set with Hood +30 defense, -25% magic damage, +7% magic critical strike chance, +8% movement speed and Lifesteal orbs spawn when dealing magic damage to an enemy. The orbs fly to the player and restore health by 20% of damage dealt. In multiplayer, the player with the lowest health on the team is healed instead.
- Crafting a one-headpiece set needs 54 Spectre Bars (27 Ectoplasm and 330 Chlorophyte Ore).
- Crafting Station: Mythril Anvil or Orichalcum Anvil.

## Beetle Armor

- Hardmode, post-Golem, melee.
- Consist of the Beetle Helmet, Beetle Leggings, and one of two chestpieces (Beetle Scale Mail for offense, Beetle Shell for defense).
- Full set with Scale Mail grants stacking "Beetle Might" buff (up to +30% melee damage/speed) the longer the player deals damage.
- Full set with Shell grants stacking "Beetle Endurance" buff (up to 45% damage reduction) the longer the player avoids damage.
- Has the second-highest defense in the game (behind Solar Flare armor).
- Crafting a full set with one chestpiece needs 18 Beetle Husks (dropped by Golem) plus a full Turtle armor set (54 Chlorophyte Bars/270 Chlorophyte Ore and 3 Turtle Shells).
- Crafting Station: Mythril Anvil or Orichalcum Anvil.
