# Event: The Torch God

## Overview

- A unique event with no boss or enemies to kill - it's a dodging/endurance challenge.
- Torch flames shoot off as projectiles one at a time, in random order, at a rate of 3/second, dealing 40/80 (Expert)/120 (Master) base damage.
- Projectiles can pass through blocks to reach the player, but can collide with blocks again after passing.
- Each torch fires once then extinguishes itself (like being switched off via wire).
- The player is inflicted with the Blackout debuff for the entire event, significantly reducing field of vision.
- Ends once all nearby torches have fired, up to a maximum of 200 attacks.
- If at least 95 torches fired, the player receives the Torch God's Favor - lets torches auto-match the biome they're placed in, and matching torches boost Luck.
- All torches re-light when the event ends (unless the player left the world mid-event, in which case they stay unlit and must be reactivated/replaced).
- Dying or leaving the underground area ends the event early with no reward; moving into a torch-less area also ends it early, but the Favor is still awarded if 95+ torches already fired.
- 60-second cooldown after the event ends before it can start again for that player.
- In multiplayer, only one player is targeted at a time.
- Torch fireballs don't harm critters, enemies, or players who've already consumed the Favor.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: Massively increased damage (80 in Expert and 120 in Master mode per fireball), plunging your screen into a partial blackout where 101 clustered underground torches turn into rapid-fire projectiles.

## How It Triggered

- Requires at least 101 torches of any type within a 41x41-tile area centered on the player, while below the surface layer.
- Any combination of torch types works.
- Cannot trigger for a character who has already consumed the Torch God's Favor or has one in their inventory (trash slot, storage, or dropped on the ground doesn't count as "in inventory," so this can be worked around to repeat the event).
- It's possible to trigger with slightly fewer than 101 torches if moving, due to a counting quirk.

## Enemies

- None - this event has no combat. The only threats are unrelated Underground/Cavern enemies that might spawn nearby and add extra danger while you're dodging.

## Preparation

- Terrain: clear out a decent open underground space with Bombs/Dynamite, then smooth it out with a pickaxe. Open space makes dodging much easier.
- Lay down Rope and/or Platforms so you can circle the torch cluster; note that descending through multiple platforms briefly costs vertical momentum, so fewer platforms can be better.
- Place torches around the outside of the arena - spreading them out a bit makes them easier to remove later if needed, at the cost of the event taking slightly longer to trigger.
- Torch visibility varies: Crimson Torches are almost invisible, while Ultrabright and White Torches are the brightest and easiest to dodge by.
- Campfires, Glowsticks, lanterns, and chandeliers do NOT count as torches for the event, so they're safe to use for extra lighting/regen without adding to the count. Peace Candles also don't count and reduce enemy spawns.
- Aim for ~200 HP minimum.

## Recommended Armor

- Ultrabright Helmet (great vision, only 4 defense)
- Gold/Platinum (16-20 defense)
- Shadow/Crimson armor (19 defense + speed bonus)
- Gladiator armor (knockback immunity).

## Recommended Accessories

- Frog Leg (auto-jump + extra safe fall distance)
- In Expert+, Shield of Cthulhu or Brain of Confusion can straight-up prevent damage
- Worm Scarf reduces damage taken
- running boots (Hermes/Flurry) and extra-jump accessories (Cloud/Sandstorm in a Bottle) help with dodging
- Shiny Red Balloon stacks with most jump accessories
- Lucky Horseshoe prevents fall damage in a tall arena
- Magiluminescence and Panic Necklace both help with speed.
- Mounts: Chillet (dash through flames) or Slime Mount, both good for dodging.
- Hooks: any grappling hook helps, but Ivy Whip, Fish Hook, and Slime Hook are best; Gem Hooks are also solid if available.

## Recommended Potions

- Calming Potion (fewer enemy spawns)
- Night Owl Potion (see projectiles better)
- Ironskin Potion (reduce damage)
- food buffs (defense + speed + full regen in Expert+)
- Swiftness Potion (mobility).

## Arena Setup

- Minecart Track loop: build a loop of Minecart Tracks (diamond, hexagon, whatever shape) around a central torch cluster, hammer the end tiles into bumpers to reverse direction, and just ride the loop to dodge automatically. Test the track before starting the event.
- Single-direction strategy: place all torches in the center of a large open arena, then just keep moving in one direction near the edges for the duration.
- Vertical hellevator strategy: rope line down a hellevator with a torch every 10 tiles and 101 torches at the top - by the time you reach the bottom, you should have your Favor.
- Circling strategy: small square arena with torches clustered in the center, Ropes/walkways around them to circle continuously.

## Strategies

- Enable Autopause - it lets you plan your next dodge every time you check your inventory.
- Once the Dryad has moved in, buy Grass Seeds and Sunflowers to plant underground - reduces enemy spawns and gives a small speed boost.
- Minions (Finch, Flinx, Vampire Frog Staff, etc.) can passively clean up random enemy spawns while you focus on dodging, regardless of your class.
- Decide whether you're treating this as a mobility challenge (prioritize dodging gear) or an endurance challenge (prioritize defense and just tank hits) - the gear recommendations above split along those lines.
- Placing the Nurse in a house near the arena means you never have to dodge at all (the Torch God won't harm town NPCs) - just eat the hits and heal, provided you have money and a grappling hook to avoid being knocked around.
