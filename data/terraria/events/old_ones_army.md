# Event: Old One's Army

## Overview

- A unique Dungeon Defenders 2 crossover event triggered by placing an Eternia Crystal on an Eternia Crystal Stand.
- Waves of enemies spawn from portals on both sides of the crystal, trying to destroy it - not the player.
- If the crystal is destroyed, the event automatically ends in failure (no Defender Medals, though bosses defeated this way still drop their unique loot).
- Three tiers of increasing difficulty:
  - Tier 1: 5 waves, Dark Mage mini-boss, 5 Defender Medals total.
  - Tier 2: 7 waves, Ogre mini-boss, 25 Defender Medals total.
  - Tier 3: 7 waves, Betsy as final boss, 100 Defender Medals total (+30-49 bonus from Betsy's Treasure Bag in Expert Mode; Master Mode boss relics only drop in this tier).
- At the end of each wave there's a 30-second countdown; interacting with the Eternia Crystal Stand during this time skips straight to the next wave.
- Each enemy counts as 1 kill in Classic Mode, or 2 kills in Expert Mode+.
- Anti-cheat mechanisms during the event: can't place/mine blocks (Creative Shock debuff), enemies go "ghostly" and phase through blocks with no clear path to the player, Teleporters don't affect them, lava does no damage to them.
- The Eternia Crystal has 2x health in Expert Mode, 3x in Master Mode, and regenerates slowly when not taking damage.
- Defeating any tier isn't required for progression, but is the only way to access Defender Medals, Tavernkeep gear, and sentry summon weapons.
- A message "The old one's army is approaching!" appear when the event starts.
- A message "The old one's army has been defeated!" when the event ends.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Triggered

- Requires the Tavernkeep NPC, who appears as the Unconscious Man after the Eater of Worlds or Brain of Cthulhu is defeated.
- Buy the Eternia Crystal Stand and an Eternia Crystal from the Tavernkeep, place the Stand, then place the Crystal on it.
- Terrain requirements for the Stand:
  - Minimum 60 tiles of horizontal clearing on each side (121 tiles total), though portals can spawn up to 120 tiles away on each side if given the space.
  - Minimum 10 tiles of vertical clearance.
  - Max 3 tiles of vertical deviation in the floor, or the Crystal is rejected.
  - Floor holes can't be wider than 1 block; platforms/inactive blocks/holes don't count as flat floor.
- Tier triggers automatically based on progression when summoned:
  - Tier 1: Before any mechanical boss has been defeated.
  - Tier 2: In a Hardmode world, after at least one mechanical boss has been defeated.
  - Tier 3: After Golem has been defeated.
- Bringing a mechanical boss's summon item to a Pre-Hardmode world and beating it there still only unlocks Tier 1.

## Enemies

- Tier 1:
  - Etherian Goblin - basic, most common enemy; low stats, no special attack.
  - Etherian Goblin Bomber and Etherian Javelin Thrower - stronger ranged enemies, start Waves 2 and 3.
  - Etherian Wyvern - highly damaging flyer that dives at the Crystal; starts Wave 4.
  - Dark Mage - mini-boss, spawns Wave 5; alternates shooting magic energy, summoning Old One's Skeletons, and healing nearby enemies. At least one must die to end the event.
- Tier 2: (T1 enemies return with much higher stats and new designs, plus:)
  - Kobold - dashes at the player/Crystal and explodes on contact for high splash damage.
  - Kobold Glider - a flying Kobold variant with much lower stats.
  - Drakin - high health/defense/knockback immunity, slow, fires magic shots from range.
  - Wither Beast - surrounds itself with crystals inflicting Withered Armor (halves defense), high health, regenerates.
  - Ogre - mini-boss; hurls snot balls (inflicts Oozed, slows), jumps for a shockwave landing, and clubs at close range. Must be the last kill to end the event; drops a unique accessory or weapon.
- Tier 3: (T1/T2 enemies return with even higher stats, plus:)
  - Etherian Lightning Bug - flies at the Crystal, inflicts Withered Weapon on hit (halves damage output temporarily).
  - Betsy - final boss, spawns Wave 7; unlike other enemies, actively targets the player rather than the Crystal. Attacks: circling before charging, fire breath, fireballs, and summoning Etherian Wyverns. Must be defeated to end the event.

## Preparation

- Build your arena flat before starting - no blocks can be placed or removed once the event begins.
- Blocking an enemy's path doesn't stop it - they phase through obstacles to reach the Crystal, so a flat, open arena beats walls.
- Asphalt Blocks on the floor speed up player movement.
- Campfires, Heart Lanterns, Honey, Heart Statues, and Bast Statues around the arena help you survive longer.
- Build a raised platform ~20 blocks up, roughly halfway between the Crystal and where the portals will spawn - good for Flameburst Towers, Ballistas, or other sentries that need to hit flyers.
- You get 10 Etherian Mana at the start (enough for one Tavernkeep sentry); enemies drop more as they die. All Etherian Mana disappears once the event ends.
- Keep moving between both sides of the Crystal - it's easy to get overwhelmed if enemies from one side go unchecked while you focus the other.
- Tier 1: Watch for Etherian Wyverns - they close distance fast and hit hard; sentries are weaker against flyers than grounded enemies. A common combo: close-range sentry (Lightning Aura/Explosive Trap) at each portal, long-range sentry (Ballista/Flameburst) closer to the Crystal.
- Tier 2: Grinding out Tier 1 first (before beating a mechanical boss) lets you save up for a Tier 2 sentry and full Tavernkeep armor set ahead of time. All classes should use the War Table from Tier 1 for an extra sentry slot.
- Tier 3: Grinding Tier 2 first (before Golem) is similarly beneficial. Reforge accessories to Menacing to maximize sentry damage - Warding is much less useful here since the goal is defending the Crystal, not yourself.
- Sentry placement (holds across all tiers): 2 Ballistas behind each portal (to make use of pierce) + rest near the Crystal; 2-4 Lightning Auras in a line at each portal's max range; Explosive Traps in a line in front of each portal; Flamebursts placed close to the Crystal.

## Arena Setup

- Make a flat area around 125 blocks or longer. The portal spawns up to 120 blocks away from the center 120 blocks away from the center. A longer arena (around 240+ blocks total) is highly recommended. It gives you significantly more time to kill enemies before they reach your crystal.
- Place your Eternia Crystal Stand in the middle.
- 10 tiles of completely clear vertical space above the floor is a must.
- Place Campfires, Heart Lanterns, and Star in a Bottles for health and mana regeneration boost.

## Strategies

- Build a second platform on top of the Crystal.
- Stay elevated on platforms - most enemies fall through trying to reach you, neutralizing them while letting you dodge ranged attacks.
- Keep moving between both sides of the Crystal so neither portal goes unchecked.
- Watch for flyers (Wyverns, Kobold Gliders, Lightning Bugs) - sentries are weak against them, so plan separate coverage (elevated Ballista/Flameburst, or a homing weapon).
- On Tier 3, build a second layer of platform if you haven't yet. This upper layer acts as a physical shield, completely blocking Betsy's devastating, downward-sweeping aerial flamethrower attacks.
