# Event: Frost Legion

## Overview

- A Hardmode event with unique snowman enemies swarming player's spawn point, similar to Goblin Army.
- Defeated after a certain amount of event enemeis killed.
- Using "Kill count = 80 + 40 x Player count" for total amount of enemy.
- For each player has at least 200 max health, an additional 40 Snowmen spawn. Thus a single player needs to defeat 120 Snowmen.
- If eight qualifying players are present, they will have to defeat 400 Snowmen.
- The largest possible Frost Legion is 10,280 Snowmen, for a game with 255 qualifying players.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Triggered

- Can only be summonsed using Snow Globe, which aquired from Presents during christmas event (December 15-31 or December 1- January 15).
- After using Snow Globe, a message appear "The Frost Legion is approaching from the west!" or "The Frost Legion is approaching from the east!".
- A message "The Frost Legion has arrived!" appear when the event starts.
- "The Frost Legion has been defeated!" shows up once the event over.
- Defeating it unlocks the Santa Claus (NPC).

## Enemies

- Mister Stabby
- Snowman Gansta
- Snow Balla

## Preparation

- One of the few events that can't be stopped without completing it - securing your spawn point is critical, especially Mediumcore/Hardcore.
- Set your spawn via a Bed underground, far from other NPCs, or high in the air where enemies can't spawn, to avoid being spawnkilled.
- Stock a chest or Piggy Bank near spawn with backup weapons, blocks, and potions.
- They are tougher than Goblin Army, but no caster or flying units.

## Recommended Weapons

- Depending on your latest boss preparation, but Piercing, Crowd-Clearing and AoE weapons are highly recommended.

## Recommend Potions

- Depending on your latest boss preparation.
- Any potions that boost stats, defenses, movement and regeneration:
  - Ironskin Potions
  - Regeneration Potions
  - Swiftness Potion
- Drink Battle Potion for maximizing enemy spawn, boosting the speed of the event, but you need to fight more enemy at once.
- Extra like Rage/ Wrath, Archery, Magic Power/Mana Regenration, and summoning are good too.
