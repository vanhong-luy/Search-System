# Event: Pirate Invasion

## Overview

- A Hardmode invasion event where pirate-themed enemies spawn in place of regular enemy spawns.
- It moves from a random side of the world toward the world spawn point, and defeating it at least once fulfills the requirement for the Pirate NPC to move in.
- A message "Pirates are approaching from the west!" or "Pirates are approaching from the east!" will appear if spawn naturally
- A message "The pirates have arrived!" appear when the event starts.
- A message "The pirates have been defeated!" when the event ends.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Triggered

- Manually: Use a Pirate Map, provided at least one player has 200+ max health and no invasion is already active.
- Randomly at dawn (4:30 AM), if all of these are true:
  - No other invasion or the Old One's Army is active
  - The Goblin Army event fails to occur that dawn
  - The world is in Hardmode
  - At least one Altar has been destroyed
  - At least one player has 200+ max health
- Odds: ~3.28% per dawn for the first Pirate Invasion, ~1.64% for subsequent ones (after the Goblin Army first fails to trigger, which itself has a 98.33% chance).
- Required kill count to complete: `120 + 60 x player count` (180 in singleplayer).

## Enemies

- Parrot (flies around, contact damage only, doesn't count toward event kill total)
- Pirate Deckhand (unarmed, melee/contact damage only)
- Pirate Corsair (sword-wielding melee, moves fast, drops more loot than lower-tier enemies)
- Pirate Crossbower (ranged, fires flaming arrows with a debuff - dangerous in the open)
- Pirate Deadeye (ranged gun, very inaccurate but still worth avoiding)
- Pirate Captain (gun + explosive cannon, huge loot, only one active at a time)
- Pirate's Curse (spawns when a Captain dies, flies through blocks, trap-immune - main threat to players hiding in shelters)
- Flying Dutchman (mini-boss, four destructible cannons, best loot in the event) |

## Preparation

- Don't fight in the open early in Hardmode - build an enclosed box or dig a tunnel under a flat arena with platform rows above. Enemies (except Pirate's Curse) can't reach you inside, and you fire through small holes or with weapons that hit through blocks.

## Arena Setup

- Your NPC house is enough.
- Use your boss arena is fine too.

## Strategies

Tunnel/Box Strategy: Dig a tunnel beneath a flat arena with rows of platforms above. Hide underground to avoid all damage except from the Pirate's Curse, and fight through blocks with Flint, Daedalus Stormbow, Anchor, Crystal Vile Shard, or minions. Use the platforms (with Queen Spider Staff or Barnacle Staff) to reach the Flying Dutchman when it arrives.

Airborne Box: Build an elevated box shelter - with the right setup, the Flying Dutchman will ram into it and expose itself to attack.

Handling the Pirate's Curse: Since it flies through walls and is trap-immune, keep a backup melee weapon and prioritize high knockback to keep it off you (a Titan Potion helps here).

Other tips:

- No pirate enemy pierces, so walled-off shooting is safe from everything but the Curse.
- Killing a Pirate Captain always spawns a Pirate's Curse - be ready.
- Flask of Gold can net 45-55 gold coins per event, making this a solid money farm.
- Fighting in a specific biome (or an artificial one near spawn) lets you farm that biome's Biome Keys.
- Fighting pirates in the Ocean biome can drop more Pirate Maps for easier re-farming.
- Trapping the Flying Dutchman creates an indefinite enemy source - good for money farming with a Slime Staff + Lucky Coin.
