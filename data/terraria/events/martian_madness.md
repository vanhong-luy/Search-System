# Event: Martian Madness

## Overview

- A Hardmode invasion available after defeating Golem, featuring unique alien-themed enemies.
- It's completed by hitting a required kill count, tracked with a progress bar, and rewards powerful loot from its boss, the Martian Saucer - Rewards include the Xenopopper, Xeno Staff, Laser Machinegun, Electrosphere Launcher, Influx Waver, and Cosmic Car Key.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Triggered

- Triggered by a Martian Probe, which spawns randomly in the outer lateral thirds of the world (higher chance in the Space layer). If the Probe detects a player and successfully escapes, the event begins.
- Cannot be summoned using item
- Requires: no other invasion active, and at least one player with 200+ max health.
- Unlike other invasions, it starts right at the world spawn point - enemies spawn near spawn or near town NPCs.
- Required kill count: `160 + 40 x player count` (200 in singleplayer). All enemies count as 1 kill except Tesla Turrets (0) and the Martian Saucer (10).
- Martian Saucers can only spawn after 30% event progress, and become much more common after 50%.

## Enemies

- Gray Grunt (basic enemy, deceptively dangerous - very high health and total knockback immunity)
- Ray Gunner (similar to Gray Grunt but weaker, shoots lasers; can ride a Scutlix as a "Scutlix Gunner" - fast, high contact damage, drops the Brain Scrambler item)
- Martian Walker (fast, high health, knockback-immune, shoots lasers, but gets stuck on obstacles due to size and weak jump)
- Martian Drone (small, low stats, locks on and charges into the player before exploding)
- Gigazapper (like a weaker Gray Grunt, inflicts Electrified debuff on hit - rapid health loss while moving)
- Martian Engineer (low stats, deploys Tesla Turrets that inflict Electrified - kill quickly before it overwhelms you)
- Martian Officer (spawns with a Bubble Shield that must be destroyed first, and reactivates if not finished off quickly)
- Brain Scrambler (like a Ray Gunner, inflicts Confused debuff)
- Martian Saucer (event boss, cycles between turret lasers, homing missiles, and a chasing Deathray; destroy the Turrets and Cannon before its Core becomes vulnerable, at which point it becomes far more aggressive)

## Preparation

Terrain: Fight near your base rather than in the open - the sheer number of high-stat/projectile enemies can easily overwhelm you outdoors. Protect your NPCs, as they can be killed easily. Piercing weapons or summons let you hide in a room/underground against the weaker (wall-blocked) enemies, but the Saucer's Deathray goes through blocks, so pair this with an escape plan - flying mounts (Witch's Broom, Gelatinous Pillion) let you outrun ground enemies until they despawn.

- Melee: Flairon/Sky Dragon's Fury, Flying Dragon (through-wall projectile)/Death Sickle, North Pole/Christmas Tree Sword, Seedler/Terra Blade, Paladin's Hammer + Possessed Hatchet, Eye of Cthulhu yoyo, Vampire Knives, Scourge of the Corruptor.
- Ranged: Candy Corn Rifle/Chain Gun with Crystal or Chlorophyte Bullets, Sniper Rifle w/ Endless Musket Pouch, Tactical Shotgun, Aerial Bane, Snowman Cannon, Piranha Gun.
- Magic: Blizzard Staff, Razorblade Typhoon/Nightglow, Razorpine/Bubble Gun, Staff of Earth, Rainbow Gun/Magnet Sphere/Golden Shower, Betsy's Wrath.
- Summoner: Tempest Staff/Deadly Sphere Staff/Raven Staff (or Terraprisma if obtained), Electric Eel/Dark Harvest/Kaleidoscope/Morning Star whips, Staff of the Frost Hydra/Ballista Staff sentries.
- Depending on your latest boss preparation, but Piercing, Crowd-Clearing and AoE weapons are highly recommended.

Accessories: Wings (Fishron Wings/Hoverboard) with Frog Leg for faster ascent, Master Ninja Gear, Celestial Shell/Stone, damage boosters (Destroyer Emblem, Magic Quiver, Mechanical Glove), Charm of Myths.

## Strategies

Circle and Shake (Expert/Master): Using a flying mount (from Duke Fishron or Mourning Wood), stay above the Saucer during its side-laser attack. As it fires homing rockets, circle down and underneath, coming up the other side to dodge - this positions you to fly away from its downward laser. Past 50% health it becomes an aggressive chaser: flee only until its laser turns off, then reverse direction each cycle. Watch for burning Saucer Scrap debris knocking you into the laser - knockback-canceling accessories (Cobalt/Hero Shield) help.

Too Fast to Catch (Master): Build a long lava-coated bridge (with Lava Waders/Obsidian Skin Potion) to prevent enemy spawns during the fight. Use the Witch's Broom to dodge phase 1 (circle the saucer for lasers/rockets; the deathray only fires straight down, so stay above or beside it, never underneath). In phase 2, switch to the Hexxed Branch to outrun the Saucer while firing a ranged weapon like the Venus Magnum - commit fully when passing underneath it, since slowing down means death, and equip knockback-canceling gear against Saucer Scrap debris. A 2,000+ block bridge is recommended for high-DPS phase 2 kills.

General money/farming note: Since Martian Saucers drop the best loot, using paired Portals/Teleporters (or a Magic Conch) ~500 tiles apart lets you despawn regular invasion enemies without progressing the event, maximizing Saucer spawn chances once past 30% completion.
