# Event: Goblin Army

## Overview

- An invasion event that spawns dangerous goblin enemies at a very fast rate until enough are killed.
- The invasion advances toward the center of the world from one edge, then settles there.
- New NPCs will not move in and Pylons are disabled while the event is active.
- Required kill count: 120 goblins in singleplayer, or 80 + 40 more per player with 200+ max HP in multiplayer (Goblin Scouts don't count).
- Event progress is saved when leaving and reentering the world; leaving does not make the invasion go away.

- A message "A goblin army is approaching from the west!" or "A goblin army is approaching from the east!" will appear if spawn naturally
- A message "A goblin army has arrived!" appear when the event starts.
- A message "A goblin army has been defeated!" when the event ends.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Triggered

- Requires at least one player with 200+ max HP (this also blocks manual summoning once a Shadow Orb/Crimson Heart has been smashed).
- Naturally: after a Shadow Orb/Crimson Heart is smashed, has a 1/3 chance each morning, dropping to 1/30 and later 1/60 after the invasion has been defeated before.
- Manually: use a Goblin Battle Standard (craftable at a Loom with 10 Tattered Cloth + 5 any Wood; the Standard can be used even before a Shadow Orb/Crimson Heart is broken).
- Tattered Cloth drops from Goblin Scouts, which spawn during the day in the outer 1/6ths of the world in the Forest.

## Enemies

### Pre-Hardmode:

- Goblin Warrior (most common, slowest, bulkiest/hardest-hitting, can shield archers)
- Goblin Archer (shoots arrows, falls to the back of the horde)
- Goblin Thief (fast, frail, breaks doors instead of opening them - doesn't steal items)
- Goblin Peon (fast, frail, breaks doors)
- Goblin Sorcerer (rare, teleports near the player, fires Chaos Balls that pass through blocks)

### Hardmode:

- Same Pre-Hardmode spawns continue (Sorcerers hit much harder in Expert+)
- Goblin Warlock (main threat - flies around shooting Chaos Balls and summoning Shadowflame Apparitions)

## Preparation

- One of the few events that can't be stopped without completing it - securing your spawn point is critical, especially Mediumcore/Hardcore.
- Set your spawn via a Bed underground, far from other NPCs, or high in the air where enemies can't spawn, to avoid being spawnkilled.
- Stock a chest or Piggy Bank near spawn with backup weapons, blocks, and potions.
- Goblins (except the Warlock) can't fly and fall through platforms trying to reach your height - staying elevated on platforms makes most of them harmless and lets you dodge arrows/Chaos Balls.
- Standing 2 blocks below a platform (via Hook, Rope, or low platform) causes goblins above to drop through it, letting you clear them out safely.
- Lava kills goblins but not Sorcerers (they don't move).
- Protect NPC housing: build above ground, block over doors, or remove the footing goblins need to push doors open.
- A classic farming setup: dig a pit, stand at the edge, and toss Grenades down - clears the invasion fast with minimal risk (watch for Sorcerers teleporting to your platform).

## Recommend Potions

- Depending on your latest boss preparation.
- Any potions that boost stats, defenses, movement and regeneration:
  - Ironskin Potions
  - Regeneration Potions
  - Swiftness Potion
- Drink Battle Potion for maximizing enemy spawn, boosting the speed of the event, but you need to fight more enemy at once.
- Extra like Rage/ Wrath, Archery, Magic Power/Mana Regenration, and summoning are good too.
