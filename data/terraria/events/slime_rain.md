# Event: Slime Rain

## Overview

- An Event of frequent spawns of Green, Blue, Cloud, and Purple Slimes, and rarely Pinky.
- all slimes spawned will exhibit hostile behavior towards the player, unlike naturally spawned slimes.
- Green, Blue, and Purple Slimes will also appear falling in the background.
- Defeating 150 slimes during the event will cause King Slime to spawn; if he has been defeated previously, he will spawn after only 75 slimes are defeated.
- Last around 9 minutes and 15 minutes (always 15 minutes on multiplayer), though it will end immediately when King Slime is defeated.
- 84 minutes to 3 hours cooldown.
- Will not end if the player leaves the world.
- The status message "Slime has stopped falling from the sky." appears when the event ends.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Trigered

- Player needs to have at least 140+ HP and 8+ defenses.
- Player needs to be in the world for at least 24-48 minutes.
- Randomly but can also be initiated manually at any time by throwing a Slime Crown into Shimmer.
- The beginning of the event is signaled with the status message "Slime is falling from the sky!".
- Cannot trigger when it's raining.

## Enemies

- Random chance to spawn:
  - Green Slime
  - Blue Slime
  - Purple SLime
  - Cloud Slime
  - Pinky
- King Slime (after defeat 150 or 75 slimes)
- Spiked Slime (Expert Mode, spawn from king Slime)

## Preparation

- No heavy preparation needed, as the event won't end when playre die.
- If you don't want to fight, stay indoor is the best choice.

## Recommended Weapons

- As the event is fairly easy, even when in early game, any weapon should do, but piercing preferred.

### Recommend Melee Weapons

- Thorn Chakram and Trimarang.
- Ball O' Hurt and Flaming Mace.
- Mandible Sword.
- Enchanted Sword, Starfury, and Blade of Grass.
- These weapons are perfect for this fight.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Any Bow, Gold, Platinum, Tendon or Demon Bow.
- Jester's Arrows and Unholy Arrows.

### Recommend Magic Weapons

- Gem Staves.
- Wand of Frosting.
- Thunder Zapper and Crimson Rod.
- Vilethorn.

### Recommend Summoning Weapons

- Snapthorn.
- Cowwhip and Leather Whip.
- Foxsparks.
- Flinx Staff and Vampire Frog Staff.

## Recommend Armor

- Not necessary.
- If you want to wear, any armor will do.
  - Silver or Tungsten
  - Platinum or Gold
  - Cactus Armor
- Gladiator and Ancient Shadow Armor for melee users.
- Fossil Armor with Gi is for ranged users.
- Wizard set or Jungle Armor for mages.
- Flinx Fur Coat is best for summoner.

## Recommend Accessories

- Anything would do.
- Most accessories can help. At this point, player unlikely to have access to Tinker's Workshop, so combined accessories are not revelant here.
- Boots: Hermes Boots, Duneriders Boots, Flurry Boots, or Sailfish Boots, will supports player's horizontal mobility.
- Fledgling Wings: not required, but great if obtained.
- Any double jump accessories: Cloud in a Bottle, Blizzard in a Bottle, Sandstorm in a Bottle Tsunami in a Bottle and Fart in a Jar.
- Band of Regeneration: Boost health regeneration.
- Feral Claws: great for melee and summoners
- White String: Add range to yoyos, if player use it.
- Shackle: +1 defend. it is easy to find.

## Recommend Potions

- Not necessary, but good.
  - Ironskin Potions and Regeneration: Boost defend and regen rate increase survivability.
  - Swiftness Potions: Increase mobility.
  - Ale or Sake: Best for melee users.
  - Archery Potions: For ranged users, increase bow's effectiveness
  - Mana Regeneration Potions and Magic Power Potions: very important for mages for quick mana regen and magic power.
  - Summoning Potions: for summoners to get an extra minions.

## Arena Setup

- Not need fancy arena, just your NPC house will do.
- If you fought King Slime before, use that arena is recommended for King Slime falling.
- If not, not necessary to build one, but recommended.

## Strategies

- No strategy needed.
- If you know how to attack, you're good.
