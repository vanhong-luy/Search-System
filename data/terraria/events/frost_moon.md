# Event: Frost Moon

## Overview

- A post-Plantera, christmas-themed event.
- One of the Moon event, the other being the Pumpkin Moon.
- Harder than Pumpkin Moon, yet yeild less money, but drops more powerful weapons.
- A wave-based event.
- Has a large variety of strong enemies and bosses.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Triggered

- Player must wait until midnight and use the Naughty Present (a Hardmode, post-Plantera summoning item used to trigger the Frost Moon event. It requires 20 silk (7 Cobwebs at a Loom), 5 ectoplasm (st-Plantera crafting material dropped by Dungeon Spirits in the Dungeon), and 5 souls of fright to craft at a Mythril or Orichalcum Anvil).
- Does not happen naturally, player need to summon it, from 7:30pm to 4:30am (any time at night)

## Preparation

- One of the few events that can't be stopped without completing it - securing your spawn point is critical, especially Mediumcore/Hardcore.
- Set your spawn via a Bed underground, far from other NPCs, or high in the air where enemies can't spawn, to avoid being spawnkilled.
- Stock a chest or Piggy Bank near spawn with backup weapons, blocks, and potions.

## Recommended Weapons

### Recommended Melee Weapons

- Terra Blade, Influx Waver and Seedler: Reliable for both single and multiple target.
- Flying Dragon: A stronger alternative
- Keybrand: High DPS against enemies below 40% health, need a Cross Necklace to be used efficiently. Thrown to the ground for passive damage.
- Psycho Knife: The opposite of Keybrand, stronger against enemies with higher health.
- Starlight: Safe, strong alternative.
- North Pole: Best for clearing hordes.
- Flairon: Strong against Ice Queens
- Vampire Knives: Best for quick heal, low DPS.

### Recommended Ranged Weapons

- Aerial Bane: Strong, high damage against Ice Queens.
- Tsunami or Eventide with Holy Arrows: Powerful
- Stake Launcher: Weaker alternative to Tsunami and Eventide.
- Electroshpere Launcher: Effective for crowd control.
- Stynger: Electroshpere Launcher weaker alternative.
- Xenopopper or Chain Gun with Crystal Bullets: Strongest gun available.

### Recommended Magic Weapons

- Razorblade Typhoon: Plow through waves of enemies.
- Bubble Gun, Laser Machinegun nd Charged Blaster Cannon: best against minibosses
- Nightglow: Provides a nice balance between crowd control and single target damage.
- Staff of Earth: Can be used against groups of enemies.
- Heat Ray or Bat Scepter: Can be used against bosses.
- Magnet Sphere, Rainbow Gun, Nimbus Rod, Golden Shower, and Betsy's Wrath: Very good secondary weapons.

### Recommended Summoning Weapons

- The Kaleidoscope, Electric Eel, Morning Star, and Dark Harvest: Best whips at this point in the game. They can serve specific scenarios and summoning-based playstyles.
- Xeno Staff: Extremely powerful and competent minion with consistent DPS
- Raven Staff: Xeno Staff weaker alternative.
- Tempest Staff: Slightly lower DPS and a much less competent AI, but is still viable against crowds of enemies.
- Deadly Sphere Staff: Tempest Staff weaker alternative.
- The Terraprisma: Extremely powerful and useful minion that can clear waves of enemies and weaker minibosses alike
- Desert Tiger Staff: High damage but risk leaving the player open to attack if it gets distracted.
- The Ballista Staff, Flameburst Staff, and Explosive Trap Staff: Reliable damage-dealing sentries that can be even further enhanced by wearing Tavernkeep armors.
- The Staff of the Frost Hydra: Best sentry available if you don't want to fight the Old One's Army. Its projectiles inflict Frostbite and deal relatively high damage.

## Recommend Armor

- Beetle armor: best for melee.
- Shroomite armor: Suits for rangers.
- Spectre armor used with Spectre Mask: Best for mages. A hybrid of Hallowed Headgear, Valhalla Knight's Breastplate and Dark Artist's Leggings provides comparable offensive bonuses along with rapid health regeneration.
- Spooky armor: Best for summoner.
- The sentry set bonuses from the Shinobi Infiltrator's Helmet, Valhalla Knight's Helm, Red Riding Hood and Dark Artist's Hat armors sold by the Tavernkeep shine in the moon events, as they can kill all of the grounded enemies easily.

## Recommend Potions

- Flask of Ichor: For melee users.
- Heartreach Potion is extremely helpful against the minibosses, as it will allow you to pick up their dropped hearts with no effort.
- Warmth Potion makes the Ice Queen significantly easier to deal with.
- Regeneration Potion, Endurance Potion, Lifeforce Potion, and Ironskin Potion will greatly increase survivability.
- Any food item that provides the Well Fed buff is good.
- Mana Regeneration Potion is a must for mages.
- Rage Potion and Wrath Potion will improve DPS.
- Magic Power Potion, Archery Potion, Ale or Sake and Summoning Potion will further improve it for Mages, Rangers, Melee, and Summoner users respectively.

## Arena Setup

- A flat surface about 168 blocks wide will focus all spawns on the arena.
- Above it there should be a long platform of the same width, high above the ground in order to keep grounded enemies out of reach.
- Lava pit and Traps may be helpful to take weaker enemies.
- A bed near arena, so in case of death, you can quickly get back to it.
- use your boss arena is fine too.

## Strategy

- No strategy needed. Just avoid enemies' attack and survive.
