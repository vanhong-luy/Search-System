# Event: Pumpkin Moon

## Overview

- A Hardmode, post-Plantera, Halloween-themed event summoned with the Pumpkin Moon Medallion at night, ending at dawn (4:30 AM).
- It can be triggered any time of year - the real-world Halloween season isn't required.
- Enemies spawn in 20 escalating waves, with score thresholds gating progression, and the final waves feature multiple boss-tier enemies at once.
- It's similar to (but slightly easier than) the Frost Moon.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Triggered

- Cannot occur naturally, it must be manually summoned by the player using a crafted item.
- Summoned manually with a Pumpkin Moon Medallion at night; the event ends automatically at 4:30 AM regardless of wave reached.
- Requires materials only available in Hardmode after defeating Plantera, though the event itself can technically run in earlier worlds.
- Progresses through 20 waves based on a kill-score system (each enemy is worth points; reaching the wave's score threshold advances it and resets the score to 0).
- Doesn't stack with a Blood Moon (summoning during one just gives a regular Pumpkin Moon), and summoning a Pirate Invasion during it will cancel it.
- Dying doesn't cancel the event - you respawn and can keep fighting until dawn (in singleplayer, exiting the world does cancel it).
- The wave-15 achievement requires reaching that point before dawn; the "true" goal is completing all 20 waves.

## Enemies

- Scarecrow (basic early enemy, low threat individually)
- Splinterling (early, average stats, good crowd-control targets)
- Hellhound (early-mid, average stats)
- Poltergeist (from wave 5, stronger than average enemies but not boss-tier)
- Headless Horseman (from wave 5, stronger than average, dangerous combined with minibosses)
- Mourning Wood (miniboss from wave 4, walks toward the player and fires Flaming Wood projectiles, leaves Greek Fire on the ground - easy alone but overwhelming in groups during later waves)
- Pumpking (main event boss from wave 7, hovers above the player Skeletron-style; its Flaming Scythe is the most dangerous attack - a large projectile that speeds up over time, but the boss itself is slow and easy to outrun in wide circles)

## Preparation

Terrain: Build a flat arena about 168 blocks wide to concentrate enemy spawns, with a long platform high above to keep grounded enemies out of reach. Lava pits or traps can help clear weaker enemies in early waves, though traps become a liability at higher difficulty due to low damage and invincibility-frame risk. Keep a bed nearby for quick respawns.

Armor:

- Melee: Chlorophyte Mask/Monk's Shirt/Squire's or Valhalla Knight's Greaves, or Beetle armor w/ Terra Blade
- Ranged: Shroomite armor (Leggings can be swapped for Tiki Pants or Red Riding Leggings once the set bonus falls off in later waves)
- Magic: Spectre armor with Hallowed Headgear, switching to Spectre Hood at low health
- Summoner: Tiki armor, or Squire's Great Helm post-summon for extra regen

Weapons by class:

- _Melee:_ Flairon/Scourge of the Corruptor, Influx Waver (vs. bosses)/Terra Blade (crowd control), Paladin's Hammer + Possessed Hatchet, Psycho Knife/Fetid Baghnakhs (strong vs. Mourning Wood), Starlight (post-Empress), Vampire Knives for emergency healing
- _Ranged:_ Tsunami with Holy Arrows, Electrosphere Launcher/Stynger/Pulse Bow for crowd control, Xenopopper with Crystal Bullets vs. bosses
- _Magic:_ Razorblade Typhoon (all-purpose), Bubble Gun/Laser Machinegun/Inferno Fork vs. bosses, Magnet Sphere/Rainbow Gun/Nimbus Rod as secondaries
- _Summoner:_ Xeno Staff/Tempest Staff/Deadly Sphere Staff, Ballista Staff/Lightning Aura Staff sentries, Morning Star whip (or Kaleidoscope post-Empress)

Accessories: Wings (Fishron Wings ideal, Hoverboard's hover also useful) with Frog Leg for faster ascent, Master Ninja Gear, damage boosters (Emblems, Celestial Shell/Stone, Sniper Scope, Magic Quiver), Celestial Cuffs for mana-on-hit. Modify accessories to Lucky or Menacing.

Mounts: Brain Scrambler (ride a Scutlix - fast, high jump, extra lasers) or Shrimpy Truffle/Cosmic Car Key for permanent flight, freeing up accessory slots otherwise spent on mobility.

Potions: Regeneration/Endurance/Lifeforce/Ironskin, Well Fed food, Heartreach Potion (crucial for collecting hearts from Mourning Wood/Pumpking drops), Mana Regeneration for mages, Swiftness, Rage/Wrath, class potions (Magic Power, Archery, Ale/Sake, Summoning).

## Strategies

Ichor note: Scarecrow and Splinterling are the only enemies not immune to Ichor - worth knowing if using Ichor-inflicting weapons.

Cannon platform: Building a platform 37 blocks above the ground and using a Cannon lets you deal 300 damage per cannonball to early waves (devastating even to Mourning Wood) while staying largely out of enemies' attack range.

Dynamite: In an explosion-proof arena, Dynamite's splash damage can wipe out early waves efficiently.

Diamond formation vs. Pumpking: With the Cosmic Car Key, circle Pumpking in a diamond pattern while targeting him, ideally backed by sentries (Frost Hydra, Queen Spider) to handle ground enemies.

Heart sustain: Both Mourning Wood and Pumpking drop hearts on death. In later, heavily swarmed waves, a build with Cross Necklace, Heartreach Potion, high defense, and strong damage can out-heal incoming damage purely from kill hearts.
