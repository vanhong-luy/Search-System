# Event: Blood Moon

## Overview

- An Event that takes place over an entire night, from 7:30 PM to 4:30 AM, affecting only the surface layer.
- All water in the world (even Underground/Cavern) turns blood-red for the duration, including rain and the moon itself.
- A red filter is applied to the screen while the player is on the surface.
- Spawns regular nighttime enemies plus new, more dangerous enemies in large numbers.
- Enemy spawn rate on the surface increases - enemies spawn faster, and more can exist on-screen at once than normal.
- Enemies can spawn near towns, even though towns normally suppress enemy spawns.
- Zombies and their counterparts can open Doors.
- Goldfish, Bunnies, and Penguins turn into Corrupt/Vicious variants (Corruption or Crimson world respectively) and do not revert after the event ends.
- Sleeping in a bed to skip time is disabled, but an Enchanted Sundial/Enchanted Moondial still works.
- Fishing Power is increased by 1.1x, but fishing can also hook unique enemies and the Surly Slime town pet.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Triggered

- Requires at least one player in the world with more than 120 HP.
- Can only occur on nights when the moon phase is not a new moon.
- Will not occur if the Eye of Cthulhu is set to spawn that night.
- Given those criteria, has a 1/9 (11.11%) chance of occurring each night.
- Can also be initiated manually at nighttime using a Bloody Tear (bypasses the natural criteria).
- Signaled with the status message "The Blood Moon is rising...".
- Can happen multiple nights in a row, or not happen for a long stretch - each night's roll is independent.

## Preparation

- Not much prep needed if you're prepared to just hide: get underground quickly if under-equipped - going indoors alone won't save you, since zombies can open doors and enemies swarm harder than normal.
- Simply placing a block in front of a door stops Zombies from opening it.
- Once you have decent gear, Blood Moon is a good farming opportunity for coins and unique loot (Money Trough, Shark Tooth Necklace, Top Hat, Wedding Dress/Veil, Bananarang, KO Cannon, etc.).
- A ditch around or below your base handles most ground-level enemies, including Clowns.
- Weapons with the Cursed Inferno debuff are especially strong here, since enemies are fast and you'll be dodging a lot anyway.
- Spawn-rate-increasing items (Water Candle, Battle Potion) stack with the Blood Moon's own increased spawn rate - avoid combining them if you're not ready for the extra density.
- Can be canceled early by summoning the Pumpkin Moon or Frost Moon, if things get out of hand.
