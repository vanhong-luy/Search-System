# Event: Solar Eclipse

## Overview

- A Hardmode event that begins occurring rarely after at least one mechanical boss has been defeated.
- Unique, highly aggressive enemies spawn at very high rates, even near NPCs.
- It's the longest timed event in the game - a full in-game day
- The ambient light drops to below-nighttime levels as the moon passes in front of the sun.
- Only affects the surface layer, so going underground is a way to avoid it.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Triggered

- 1/20 (5%) chance of occurring at any dawn, once at least one Mechanical Boss (Twins, Destroyer, or Skeletron Prime) has been defeated.
- Can be summoned manually with a Solar Tablet (legitimately craftable after Plantera, though Solar Tablet Fragments can be farmed from Lihzahrds outside the Jungle Temple even before that).
- Lasts from sunrise (4:30 AM) to sunset (7:30 PM) regardless of kills - there's no completion condition, it just runs the full day.
- Can be skipped with an Enchanted Sundial.
- Multiple eclipses can occur back-to-back, and it can overlap with other events like a Goblin Army or Pirate Invasion.

## Enemies

- Frankenstein / Fritz / Swamp Thing (always present, behave like slow Zombies, easy to kill)
- Creature from the Deep / The Possessed (always present, contact damage only; the former swims and leaps from water, the latter climbs walls and is knockback-immune)
- Vampire (always present, only flying enemy pre-Mechanical Bosses, hard to see in the dark, takes 10x damage from the Stake Launcher)
- Eyezor (always present, shoots lasers from range - block line of sight to neutralize)
- Reaper (appears after all 3 Mechanical Bosses are defeated, passes through blocks so box strategies don't work on it, very vulnerable to knockback, can't fly)
- Psycho / Butcher (appear after Plantera, extremely high contact damage; Psycho turns semi-invisible, Butcher charges faster over time - neither flies or phases through blocks)
- Dr. Man Fly (appears after Plantera, slow, throws poison gas clouds from range)
- Deadly Sphere (appears after Plantera, flying, passes through blocks until it closes in, then becomes solid and aggressive)
- Nailhead (appears after Plantera, very slow but shoots a screen-covering rain of nails that can kill in a few hits - high priority target)
- Mothron (appears after Plantera, mini-boss-tier, charges the player and lays eggs that hatch into Baby Mothrons - destroying eggs early stops the cycle)

## Preparation

- Early on, hiding in a tunnel just under the surface and using through-wall weapons (like Nettle Burst) works well.
- After Plantera, Mothron makes tunnels ineffective
- Switch to a large flat open arena with several rows of platforms above, plus some solid blocks to block Eyezor/Nailhead shots.

## Strategies

General: Because this is a full-day event with no kill-count completion, the focus is on survivability and consistent DPS rather than burst damage - pace your potions and use the Alchemy Flask to extend buffs.

Tunnel strategy (pre-Mothron): Stay just under the surface and fight with weapons that hit through walls.

Open arena strategy (post-Plantera): Flat ground with platform rows above for vertical mobility against Mothron, plus block cover against Eyezor and Nailhead.

Handling Mothron: Prioritize destroying its eggs before they hatch into Baby Mothrons to lock it into an egg-laying loop. Good vertical mobility (wings, Frog Leg) is essential for dodging its charges.

Money farming: Melee/Summoners can use Flask of Gold; Rangers can use Golden Bullets. All classes benefit from the Lucky Coin (especially paired with rapid-fire weapons like the Wasp Gun) for coin drops on hit. Sentry-focused summoner builds can clear large numbers of weaker enemies passively.
