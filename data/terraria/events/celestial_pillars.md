# Event: Celestial Pillars (Lunar Events)

## Overview

- Four Celestial Pillars spawn across the world:
  - Solar (Melee)
  - Vortex (Ranged)
  - Nebula (Magic)
  - Stardust (Summoner)
- Each Pillar is protected by a shield that can only be removed by defeating 100 nearby pillar enemies.
- After destroying all four Pillars, Moon Lord automatically appears after 60 seconds, leaving very little preparation time.

- On Expert Mode/Master Mode/Legendary Mode: All enemies are much harder to fight, doubling their health and damage scaling while increasing coin drops.

## How It Trigered

- Begins immediately after defeating the Lunatic Cultist.
- No summoning item, so it cannot be summoned using any item.
- A message "Celestial creatures are invading!" will pop up.

## Preparation

- Open your map and locate all four Pillars.
- Restock potions and ensure your equipment is fully prepared before defeating the Lunatic Cultist.
- The world's spawn point remains safe since no Pillars spawn there.

## Recommended Weapons

### Recommend Melee Weapons

- Flying Dragon: Powerful projectile that pierces enemies and can attack through walls, making crowd control easier.
- Influx Waver: Excellent single-target damage against the Pillars themselves.
- Christmas Tree Sword: Rains ornaments that clear groups of flying enemies.
- Terra Blade: Reliable ranged melee option with good overall DPS.
- Vampire Knives: Useful for healing between enemy waves.
- Solar Eruption / Daybreak (after Solar Pillar): Solar Eruption pierces through walls, while Daybreak excels against Moon Lord.

### Recommend Ranged Weapons

- Xenopopper + Crystal Bullets: Extremely high DPS against dense enemy groups.
- Aerial Bane: Explosive arrows devastate flying enemies.
- Tsunami / Eventide: Strong bows with excellent crowd control.
- Electrosphere Launcher: Clears clusters of ground enemies quickly.
- Vortex Beater / Phantasm (after Vortex Pillar): Powerful endgame ranged upgrades.

### Recommended Magic Weapons

- Razorblade Typhoon: Homing projectiles let you focus on dodging.
- Razorpine / Blizzard Staff: High sustained DPS.
- Nightglow: Excellent against both Pillars and stronger enemies.
- Betsy's Wrath: Reduces enemy defense while dealing solid damage.
- Nebula Arcanum / Nebula Blaze (after Nebula Pillar): Outstanding endgame magic weapons.

### Recommended Summoning Weapons

- Xeno Staff: Accurate against fast-moving enemies.
- Tempest Staff: Strong alternative with good damage.
- Terraprisma: Best summon weapon if already obtained.
- Kaleidoscope / Electric Eel: Strong whips to boost minion damage.
- Stardust Dragon Staff (after Stardust Pillar): One of the strongest summon weapons in the game.

## Recommend Armor

- Beetle Armor: High defense and survivability for melee.
  = Shroomite Armor: Highest ranged damage.
- Spectre Armor: Strong magic damage with healing capability.
- Spooky Armor: Best offensive armor for summoners.
- Hybrid Sets: Combine damage with regeneration if survivability is preferred.

## Recommend Accessories

- Fishron Wings / Betsy's Wings: Superior flight for dodging large enemy groups.
- Master Ninja Gear: Dash greatly improves survivability.
- Celestial Shell: Boosts nearly every important stat.
- Destroyer Emblem: Increases overall damage.
- Fire Gauntlet / Magic Quiver: Class-specific damage boosts.
- Charm of Myths: Shorter potion cooldown for more frequent healing.
- Shiny Stone: Quickly regenerates health when out of combat.
- Frozen Shield (Melee): Extra defense plus damage reduction below half health.

## Recommend Potions

- Ironskin Potion: Increases defense.
- Regeneration Potion: Faster health recovery.
- Endurance Potion: Reduces incoming damage.
- Lifeforce Potion: Increases maximum HP.
- Swiftness Potion: Improves mobility.
- Wrath / Rage Potion: Increases overall DPS.
- Magic Power & Mana Regeneration Potion: Essential for mages.
- Archery Potion: Boosts bow damage.
- Summoning Potion: Adds an extra minion.
- Food Buffs: Always maintain Well Fed or better.

## Pillar Order

- There is no required order, but a common approach is:
  - Your class's Pillar first (craft stronger weapons immediately).
  - Stardust Pillar (Stardust Dragon Staff benefits every class).
  - Remaining Pillars based on preference.
  - Save the most troublesome Pillar for last if desired.

## Arena Setup

- No strict arena is required, as they could spawn randomly across the world.

## Strategy

- Keep moving to avoid being surrounded.
- Focus on dangerous enemies before attacking the Pillar.
- Once the shield drops, destroy the Pillar quickly to stop enemy spawns.
- After the fourth Pillar, immediately prepare for the Moon Lord, who arrives in 60 seconds.

### Solar

- Stay mostly on the ground to avoid provoking Crawltipedes.
- Avoid shooting spinning Selenians.
- Fight behind cover when possible, since only a few enemies can pass through blocks.

### Vortex

- Prioritize Alien Queens before they spawn more enemies.
- Keep moving vertically to dodge projectiles.
- A small underground tunnel allows you to attack safely while avoiding most enemies.

### Nebula

- Never remain stationary.
- Constant movement is the safest way to avoid homing attacks.
- Eliminate Floaters first whenever possible.

### Stardust

- Build 2-block-thick barriers to block most projectiles.
- Keep your distance from the Pillar to avoid enemies spawning directly above you.
- Take advantage of Star Cells repeatedly splitting to remove the shield faster.
